\paragraph{Postulate v4.10.0-r67.P (counting postulate; adopted by the author on 26 November 2026, not derived).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(C1)] \emph{Weight.}  In the matter frame M every spin \(j\in\{\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\dots\}\) of a puncture counts with the weight \(2\) (the two
extremal states \(m=\pm j\); the U(1) counting of Domagala--Lewandowski and Meissner).
\item[(C2)] \emph{Area.}  The area of a puncture of spin \(j\) is proportional to \(\sqrt{j(j+1)}\) (Casimir spectrum).
\item[(C3)] \emph{Unit.}  The frame T counts only \(j=\tfrac12\), and one such puncture carries one bit.
\end{enumerate}
The assignment of the two rules to the frames M and T (v4.9.0-r63) is part of the postulate.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r67.1 (the number that follows; H165 K-a, not blind).}
Under r67.P, with \(k=2j\) and \(c_k=\sqrt{k(k+2)/3}\) (area in units of the area of \(j=\tfrac12\)), \(\gamma_L\) is the unique positive root of
\[
Z(\gamma_L)=\sum_{k\ge1}2^{\,1-\gamma_L c_k}=1 ,
\]
and equals \(\gamma_M/\gamma_T\) with \(\gamma_T=\ln2/(\pi\sqrt3)\).  Numerically
\[
\gamma_L=1.8646997646,\qquad
\beta_{\rm sym}=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma_L-1}{\gamma_L+1}}=0.5494056410,\qquad
\varepsilon=\operatorname{artanh}\beta_{\rm sym}=0.6175295849 .
\]
If (C2) is replaced by the semiclassical area \(j+\tfrac12\) in both frames, the sum is geometric, \(2x^2/(1-x)=1\) with \(x=2^{-\gamma_L/2}\), and
\(\gamma_L=2\), \(\beta=1/\sqrt3\) exactly (k245).  \textbf{Confirmed.}
\emph{Check.} mpmath and Wolfram Language at 40 digits: the root and \(\gamma_M/\gamma_T\) agree to 39 digits (numerical residue, not a symbolic
one); \(Z\) is strictly decreasing; the geometric case is symbolic.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r67.3 (the overlap closure forces the Hopf value; H165 K-b, not blind).}
In reading C of r66, let spin \(j\) be carried by \(2j\) symmetrised qubits with the coherent states \(|j;n\rangle=|n\rangle^{\otimes2j}\).  For the
pair \((K_1,S)\) of r66.2, \(|\langle j;K_1|j;S\rangle|=\beta^{2j}\), and
\[
\sum_{j\ge1/2}2\,|\langle j;K_1|j;S\rangle|^2=\frac{2\beta^2}{1-\beta^2}=\gamma_L-1
\]
for every \(\beta\) (the second equality was found after the advance note and is marked as such).  The closure condition ``sum \(=1\)'' holds exactly
at \(\beta^2=\tfrac13\), that is \(\gamma_L=2\).  At the reference value the sum is \(0.8647\).  The four other closures fixed in the advance note
give \(\beta=\tfrac13\) (amplitudes), \(0.7598\) (fourth powers), \(0.5240\) (exponent \(c_k\)) and \(0.6886\) (exponent \(\sqrt{k(k+2)}\)).  None
reproduces the reference value; the nearest misses by \(4.6\,\%\).  H165 K-b \textbf{refuted}, as predicted.
\emph{Reading.}  The two-readout picture, taken by itself, yields the Hopf value.  The reference value needs the Casimir area (C2) and the
one-bit unit (C3).  At spin \(\tfrac12\) the two weights are \(2^{1-\gamma_L}=0.549161\) (counting) and \(2\beta^2=0.603693\) (overlap).
\emph{Check.} Qiskit \texttt{quantum\_info} for \(2j=1,\dots,6\); sympy; Wolfram Language (symbolic zero residue for the identity).

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r67.4 (number candidates and the cone; H165 K-c, K-d).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Of 13 comparisons fixed in advance none is an identity (criterion: relative \(10^{-8}\) at 30 digits).  Two unexplained near
coincidences remain: \(2^{1-\gamma_L}=0.549161\) against \(\beta_{\rm sym}=0.549406\) (\(-4.5\cdot10^{-4}\); the self-consistent root of
\(2^{1-g}=\sqrt{(g-1)/(g+1)}\) is \(g=1.864293\)), and \(1/\langle c\rangle=0.658150\) against \(\operatorname{artanh}(1/\sqrt3)=0.658479\)
(\(-5.0\cdot10^{-4}\)), with \(\langle c\rangle=\sum_kp_kc_k\), \(p_k=2^{1-\gamma_Lc_k}\).  Both were known or estimated before the run (not blind) and
both are excluded as identities.  They are not used as support.  The four blind comparisons all miss.
\item[(b)] \(1/\sqrt3=j/\sqrt{j(j+1)}\) at \(j=\tfrac12\): the Hopf value is the cosine of the cone angle of a spin \(\tfrac12\) in the vector model, and
twice this angle is the tetrahedral angle \(\arccos(-\tfrac13)\) of r66.5.  The factor \(\sqrt3\) in \(c_k\) and in \(\gamma_T\) is the same number.
\end{enumerate}
H165 overall: \textbf{refuted}.  No closure condition and no number candidate fixes \(\beta_{\rm sym}\) without the counting rule.
