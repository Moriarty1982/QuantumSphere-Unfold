\subsection*{v4.9.0-r67 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r67-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r67.1 (which side \texorpdfstring{$\bsym^2$}{beta squared} is on).}
Theorem~\ref{thm:dark-energy} puts \(\bsym^2\) on matter: \(\Omega_M=\bsym^2\approx0.30\), \(\Omega_\Lambda=1-\bsym^2\approx0.70\).  The field equation
\(G_{\mu\nu}+\bsym^2g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu}\) of Remark~\ref{rem:omega-bsym} and the statement \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}=\bsym^2\) of
Remark~\ref{rem:einstein-blunder} put the same \(\bsym^2\) on \(\Lambda\).  Both cannot hold: the remarks give \(\Lambda\) the matter share.  This is separate
from the dimensional problem of v4.8.0-r03 (F3, F4), which concerns the same expressions.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r67.2 (counting form of the fractions).}
With the two countings of r63.1, \(\gamma_T=\ln2/(\pi\sqrt3)\) (spin \(\tfrac12\) only) and \(\gamma_M=0.2375330\) (all spins, weight \(2\)), and
\(\gL=\gamma_M/\gamma_T\),
\[
\Omega_M=\bsym^2=\frac{\gamma_M-\gamma_T}{\gamma_M+\gamma_T}=0.301847,
\qquad
\Omega_\Lambda=1-\bsym^2=\frac{2\gamma_T}{\gamma_M+\gamma_T}=0.698153 .
\]
The denominator \(\gamma_M+\gamma_T=0.364917\) is \(|\gamma_3|\) of Theorem~\ref{thm:triolic-gBI}.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r67.3 (epoch).}
\(\bsym\) is fixed under the counting reading (v4.9.0-r66.2), while \(\Omega_M\) and \(\Omega_\Lambda\) change with cosmic time.  The identification of
Theorem~\ref{thm:dark-energy} can hold at one epoch only; at every epoch it would give \(\Omega_\Lambda=0.698\) also at recombination, which is excluded.
This agrees with the present-epoch reading of v4.9.0-r15 and leaves the coincidence problem (why now) open.

\paragraph{Observation v4.9.0-r67.4 (after the fact).}
\(\Omega_M=0.3018\) against DESI DR2 BAO alone, \(0.2975\pm0.0086\): \(+0.5\sigma\); against Planck 2018 (TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing), \(0.3153\pm0.0073\):
\(-1.8\sigma\).  Remark~\ref{rem:omega-bsym} quotes \(0.3111\), a Planck+BAO value.  No test: the identification predates neither data set as a registered
prediction.

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
r67.1 by reading the three statements; r67.2 from \(\bsym^2=(\gL-1)/(\gL+1)\); r67.3 from r66.2 and the expansion history used in v4.9.0-r17; r67.4 from the
quoted values.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Reading.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Under v4.9.0-r64 the frames differ in their counting, not in \(G_N\) or the geometry.  \(\Lambda\) as a curvature is then frame-independent like \(G_N\):
the cosmological constant is real, and the remark's claim that matter observers ``necessarily measure'' it because of their frame is not supported.
\item What the framework could explain is the share \(\Omega_\Lambda\), not \(\Lambda\).  In counting form it is the share of horizon states on the minimal-spin
floor, counted twice; the matter share is what matter counts beyond the floor.  This reading is offered, not adopted.
\item The open step is the one named in v4.8.0-r03 (F11): why a share of a horizon counting should equal a share of the energy budget, and why only today.
\item Candidates for status notes: Remark~\ref{rem:einstein-blunder} and the field equation in Remark~\ref{rem:omega-bsym} (r67.1).  The decision is left to
the author.
\end{enumerate}
