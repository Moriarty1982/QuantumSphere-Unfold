(\texttt{llm/k104\_nachrechnung.py}, \texttt{llm/k105\_h46\_exakt.py}) and the literature values were looked up.  This layer records what holds and corrects two
statements.  It makes no prediction.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r68 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r68-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r68.1 (confirmed).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] The three counting solutions of r63.1 (\(0.127384\), \(0.237533\), \(0.274067\)) agree to \(10^{-12}\); Meissner (\texttt{gr-qc/0407052}) gives
\(0.23753295796592\), Ghosh and Mitra (\texttt{gr-qc/0411035}) \(\gamma\approx0.274\).
\item[(b)] Meissner counts sequences of labels \(m\), with the area depending on \(|m|\) only; the factor \(2\) counts \(\pm m\).  The counted states are the
states \(|j=|m|,m\rangle\), so the reading of r63.2 matches the counting.
\item[(c)] The saturation statement of r63.2 holds exactly for all states up to \(j=10\); the boost statements of r63.3 and r63.4 hold numerically; the
tachyon of Convention~F has rapidity \(-\eta+i\pi/2\) relative to R (r24.2).
\item[(d)] The counting form of r67.2 and the side swap of r67.1 hold; the corollary ``Cosmological constant revisited'' also writes
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}\sim\bsym^2\).  Planck 2018 gives \(\Omega_m=0.315\pm0.007\), DESI DR2 BAO \(0.2975\pm0.0086\).
\item[(e)] H46 with the unrounded \(\bsym=0.549406\) instead of \(0.5493\): \(\sin^2\theta_{23}\in[0.4908,0.4975]\) instead of \([0.4913,0.4975]\).  The registered
interval \([0.49,0.50]\) still holds; a control run with \(0.5493\) reproduces \texttt{llm/k100\_out.txt}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Correction v4.9.0-r68.2.}
The statement of r67.2 that \(\gamma_M+\gamma_T\) is \(|\gamma_3|\) of Theorem~\ref{thm:triolic-gBI} is not a finding: that theorem defines
\(\gamma_3:=-(\gstring+\gamma_{\mathrm{LQG}})\).  It holds by definition, up to the rounding of the input \(0.2375\).

\paragraph{Correction v4.9.0-r68.3 (which counting the literature prefers).}
The SU(2)-invariant treatment of the horizon (Engle, Noui, Perez, \texttt{arXiv:0905.3168}) is regarded as better founded than the U(1) treatment, which counts
too many microstates (logarithmic correction \(-\tfrac12\) instead of \(-\tfrac32\)); it gives \(\gamma\approx0.274\), the Ghosh--Mitra value.  The lepton window of
r61.1(e) selects the U(1) counting (\(0.2375\)).  Reading~5 of r63, that the data select the corrected counting, is therefore not supported: they select the counting
that the later literature considers the less well founded.  The wording ``corrected''/``superseded'' of v4.8.0-r03 (F11) reflects the state of 2004.

\paragraph{Remarks v4.9.0-r68.4.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The ABCK value and the Meissner value are both U(1) countings, so the pair of r63 is consistent within one treatment.  An SU(2) pair has the same floor
(\(2j+1=2\) at \(j=\tfrac12\)) and gives \(\bsym=0.604\), outside the window.
\item The uncertainty reading of r63.2 is a reinterpretation of the U(1) counting, not part of its derivation; mathematically it is exact.
\item ``Own frame'' of the tachyon means \(1/\bsym\) relative to R in r24.2 and the mirror frame with \(E=0\) in r63.4; the numbers agree, the words do not.
\item With the unrounded value \(\eta=0.6175\) lies near the lower edge \(0.611\) of the window of r61.1(e), so the number of H46 hits is sensitive
(\(120\to168\) in variant~A).
\end{enumerate}
