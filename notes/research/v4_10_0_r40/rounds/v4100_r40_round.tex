\paragraph{Purpose.}
Layer v4.10.0-r39 obtains the mediator term of the OP5 comparison model from one tensor field \(H_{ab}\) on the subspace \((t,x,y)\) orthogonal to the
axis, with the identically conserved coupling \(J_{ab}=\partial_a\partial_b\eta-\eta_{ab}\Box_\parallel\eta\), provided the time--transverse components \(H_{0i}\)
do not couple (\(w=0\)).  This layer tests four candidate reasons for that decoupling.  It is an exploration with a method fixed before the
computation; it is partly not blind, since candidates K1 and K4 below were sketched by hand when the method was written.  Earlier layers are
not rewritten; Remark r40.5 corrects a reading of r39.3.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r40 setting}
\label{subsec:v4100-r40-setting}

Conventions are those of r39: plane waves with \(\partial_a\to D_a\), \(D=(-i\omega,ik_1,ik_2)\), \(x=\omega^2\), \(a_\perp=k_1^2+k_2^2\),
\(J_{ab}=D_aD_b-c\,\eta_{ab}D^cD_c\), and \(d_m=m^2+r_mq-x\).  The target is the numerator \(N_m=\tfrac23(x^2-a_\perp x+a_\perp^2)\), reached in r39 by
\(N(1,0)=3N_m\).  The candidates are: K1, a massive Fierz--Pauli field on the subspace; K2, \(H_{0i}\) as auxiliary fields entering through
\(E_{ij}=\partial_tH_{ij}-\partial_iH_{0j}-\partial_jH_{0i}\) with a stiffness \(\mu\ge0\); K3, the time-reversal parity of the vertices; K4, an average over
the relative time orientation of the two ends of the exchange.  Time reversal is \(\omega\mapsto-\omega\) (complex conjugation); the tachyon map
\(\omega\mapsto i\omega\) is used only as a cross-check and is not identified with it.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r40 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r40-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r40.1 (Fierz--Pauli does not see the source).}
With the massive spin-2 projector in three dimensions, \(\Pi_{abcd}=\tfrac12(P_{ac}P_{bd}+P_{ad}P_{bc})-\tfrac12P_{ab}P_{cd}\),
\(P_{ab}=\eta_{ab}-p_ap_b/m^2\), the exchange numerator is
\[
 J\cdot\Pi\cdot J=-\frac{(c-1)\,(x-a_\perp)^2\,(a_\perp+m^2-x)\,\bigl((1-c)a_\perp+(1+3c)m^2-(1-c)x\bigr)}{2m^4}.
\]
It vanishes identically for \(c=1\) and vanishes on the pole \(x=a_\perp+m^2\) for every \(c\), so only contact terms remain.  It is never
proportional to \(N_m\).
\emph{Proof.} Direct contraction and factorization; coefficient comparison in \(x\), \(a_\perp\), \(m^2\) admits only \(\lambda=0\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r40.2 (auxiliary time components add a pole).}
Let \(H_{00}\) and \(H_{ij}\) be as in r39, with the kinetic term of \(H_{ij}\) written as \(\tfrac12E_{ij}E_{ij}\), and let \(H_{0i}\) carry no time derivative
and a stiffness term \(-\tfrac12\mu H_{0i}H_{0i}\).  Eliminating all fields at \(c=1\), the exchange differs from the r39 value \(3N_m/d_m\) by
\[
 -\frac{2a_\perp x\,d_m}{\mu\,(x-x_\mu)},\qquad x_\mu=(m^2+r_mq)\Bigl(1-\frac{2a_\perp}{\mu}\Bigr),
\]
so a finite \(\mu\) adds a second pole at \(x_\mu\).  No finite \(\mu\) removes the difference; it vanishes only in the limit \(\mu\to\infty\), where \(H_{0i}=0\)
is enforced.  At \(\mu=0\) the exchange is \(3N_m/d_m-x(m^2+r_mq)/d_m\).
\emph{Proof.} Hermitian kinetic matrix in the basis \((H_{00},H_{01},H_{02},H_{11},H_{12},H_{22})\) at \(k_2=0\), inversion, factorization.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r40.3 (time-reversal parity).}
Under \(\omega\mapsto-\omega\) the vertices \(J_{00}\) and \(J_{ij}\) are even and \(J_{0i}\) is odd, for every \(c\).  A coupling \(H^{ab}J_{ab}\) invariant under time
reversal therefore forbids \(H_{0i}J_{0i}\) exactly when \(H_{0i}\) is even, that is, when \(H\) does not transform under time reversal like a spacetime
tensor (which would make \(H_{0i}\) odd).
\emph{Proof.} \(J_{ab}(-\omega)-J_{ab}(\omega)\) is nonzero only in the \(0i\) entries, where \(J_{0i}(-\omega)=-J_{0i}(\omega)\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r40.4 (average over the relative time orientation).}
Let the exchange connect a vertex with a vertex of the same time orientation with weight \(p\) and with a time-reversed vertex with weight \(1-p\),
each component of \(H\) with positive norm (\(w=1\) in r39).  Then
\[
 p\,N_{\rm same}+(1-p)\,N_{\rm rev}=N(c,\,w=2p-1)
\]
identically, and this equals \(\lambda N_m\) exactly for \(p=\tfrac14(3c^2-2c+1)\), \(\lambda=\tfrac32(3c^2-2c+1)\).  For the conserved coupling \(c=1\) this is
\(p=\tfrac12\), \(\lambda=3\), so \(g'^2=g_m^2/3\) as in r39.  The two pure cases give \(2(x-a_\perp)^2\) (\(p=0\), the Lorentz-covariant contraction) and
\(2(x^2+a_\perp^2)\) (\(p=1\)).  The tachyon map at one vertex instead of time reversal gives a complex numerator and no cancellation.
\emph{Proof.} \(N_{\rm same}=\sum J_{ab}(\omega)^2\), \(N_{\rm rev}=\sum J_{ab}(\omega)J_{ab}(-\omega)\) over ordered pairs; by r40.3 they differ only in the sign of the
\(0i\) terms; coefficient comparison.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r40.5 (reading and correction of r39.3).}
The reading in r39.3 that Fierz--Pauli-like auxiliary time components would have no pole of their own does not hold: Fierz--Pauli on the subspace
gives zero (r40.1), and auxiliary components with finite stiffness add a pole (r40.2).  No dynamics of \(H_{0i}\) tested here produces the decoupling;
it is reached only by enforcing \(H_{0i}=0\).  It does follow exactly from a symmetry condition: by r40.4 the weight is \(w=2p-1\), so \(w=0\) means that
the exchange is even under the relative time reversal of its two ends.  This is the same time reversal (complex conjugation) that reverses the
orientation of the octant in r31.1 (H118) and that no candidate of the Companion fixes.  An orientation that is not fixed is, however, not yet an
equal weighting; the condition \(p=\tfrac12\) is named, not derived.  Against the criteria fixed beforehand the result carries \emph{partially}.
Two ends with independent time orientations would fit a string between M and T (r37, deferred idea I1); whether such a bridge carries both
orientations with equal weight is open.
