\subsection*{v4.9.0-r74 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r74-results}

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r74.1 (H58 invalid: consistent data).}
Pre-registered, not blind.  All parts of the symbol now come from the exact linearization at the repaired germ: \(J=A(0)\)
(deviation from the stored \(J\) \(1.2\times10^{-13}\)), \(\theta\) rebuilt as the linear form on the admissible 3-jets that
vanishes once the degree-3 equations are added (\(189\to188\), inclusion to \(2.8\times10^{-11}\)), the four conditions of
r73.2, and the gauge.  Without the four conditions the known dimensions \(117,\dots,898\) (\(p=2,\dots,9\)) are reproduced
with gaps \(\ge5\times10^6\).  With them, in all three frames: \(\dim g''_p=477,594,723,865\) (\(p=6,\dots,9\)), not
involutive at \(p=6,7\), involutive at \(p=8\), characters \(723,130,12,0\).  The ambiguous singular value of H57 is gone
(noise \(1.7\times10^{-12}\), smallest genuine value \(8.1\times10^{-9}\)), so \(\dim g''_8=724\) was an artifact.  Five ranks
have gap ratios \(3\times10^3\) to \(8\times10^3\) instead of the required \(10^4\); the test is invalid.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r74.2 (exploration: Spencer cohomology of the extended symbol).}
With consistent data, all \(\delta\)-rank gaps \(\ge3\times10^4\): for the gauged symbol \(g'\)
\(H^{5,2}=4\), \(H^{6,2}=H^{7,2}=0\) (check against r72.5); for the extended symbol \(g''\) \(H^{5,2}=8\), \(H^{6,2}=4\),
\(H^{7,2}=3\).  The four conditions were the only possible obstructions of the gauged system through \(k=7\); the new
sixth-order equations themselves leave room for at most four hidden conditions on 7-jets and three on 8-jets.  Whether they
occur is decided by lower-order terms, not by the symbol.

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r74.3 (method check: staged elimination and conditioning).}
A staged elimination of the linearized tower (top degree first, through left kernels of symbol-sized blocks) needs little
memory and reproduces \(371\), \(852\) and \(481\), but gives \(476\) instead of \(477\) with a gap of \(4\times10^2\).  The cause
is structural: the prolonged symbol of \(J\) alone is well conditioned (smallest genuine value \(4\times10^{-5}\), noise
\(10^{-15}\), unchanged by Ruiz equilibration), while \(\Omega\) and the four conditions are nearly dependent on the \(J\) rows
(independent weight about \(10^{-8}\)), because they carry new information only on the cone and the planes.  Double
precision separates one or two such levels, not three.

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r74.4 (exploration: plane waves on the cone).}
If the symbol module were reduced, plane waves \(\xi^p\otimes u\) on the characteristic set would span every \(g_p\) with
well-conditioned data.  Without the four conditions, 300 null directions with seven polarizations each span
\(115,184,267,364,475\) for \(p=2,\dots,6\) (gaps \(\ge2\times10^{11}\)) instead of \(117,188,273,371,481\).  The deficit grows
irregularly, which points to a non-reduced module; the simple reformulation does not carry.

\paragraph{Consequence.}
By all evidence the extended symbol is involutive first at order 8, with 12 functions of three variables.  Further hidden
conditions (up to four on 7-jets, three on 8-jets) are not excluded.  Deciding them needs either the whole chain (germ,
linearization, elimination) in higher precision or a reformulation that handles the multiplicities of the symbol module on the
characteristic set.  OP5 is not closed.
