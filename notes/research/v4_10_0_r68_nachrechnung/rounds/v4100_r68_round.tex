% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Purpose.}
Layer v4.10.0-r67 fixed \(\beta_{\rm sym}=0.5494056410\) as the reference value and stated, from the exploration k357, that no recorded verdict
depends on the difference to the value \(0.5493482\) of earlier layers.  The author asked how the individual places react.  This layer records
the answer of the exploration k358.  It is an exploration and not a test: there is no advance note and no decision criterion.  All data values
are those already in the record; none is new and none counts as a check of a prediction.  The distances in \(\sigma\) describe positions.
Earlier layers are not rewritten.  No guard changes.

\noindent\textbf{Source-only status.}
This v4.10.0-r68 layer is source-only.  It does not build a PDF, does not assign a new DOI,
and does not authorize a public release.  The v5.4.0 Version DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24500009}{10.5281/zenodo.24500009} and the Concept DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19210728}{10.5281/zenodo.19210728} are unchanged by it.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r68 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r68-results}

Three values are compared: \(0.5493\) (four digits, used by several older scripts), \(0.5493482\) (carried in earlier layers) and the reference
value.  One \emph{step} is the change \(5.74\times10^{-5}\) in \(\beta\) from the second to the third (relative \(1.05\times10^{-4}\)).
The sensitivity of a quantity \(X\) is \(s_X=d\ln X/d\ln\beta\) at the reference value.

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r68.1 (sensitivities).}
In closed form \(s_{\gamma_L}=4\beta^2/(1-\beta^4)=1.328\) and \(s_{m_W}=2\beta^2(1-\beta^2)/(1+\beta^2+\beta^4+\beta^6)=0.297\) for
\(m_W=m_Z\gamma_L/\sqrt{1+\gamma_L^2}\).  Further values: \(\beta^2\) (\(\Omega_M\) of H96, \(|U_{e2}|^2\) of H9) \(2.00\);
\(\varepsilon=\operatorname{artanh}\beta\) \(1.274\); \(\gamma_L^5\) \(6.64\); \(K=(1+\beta^4)/3\) \(0.334\); the Hopf gap
\(1/(\sqrt3\,\beta)-1\) \(-20.7\).  Only the Hopf gap and high powers of \(\gamma_L\) react strongly; neither is compared with data in the
record.  \(\gamma_L^5\) changes from \(22.5291\) to \(22.5447\) (\(+0.07\%\)).  \textbf{Computed} (mpmath; sensitivities symbolically in
Wolfram Language).

rule \(1777.134\) MeV (\(+2.27\sigma\)).  H102 was registered with the latter on 13 November 2026; its status is unchanged and open.  The
rounded value follows from no rule and is not an alternative.  \textbf{Computed} (functions of the registered script k224).
