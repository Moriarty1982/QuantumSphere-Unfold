\subsection*{v4.9.0-r50 model and data}
\label{subsec:v490-r50-model}

For \(X\in\{u,d,e,\nu\}\): \(M_X=C_X+\varepsilon(W_XC_X+C_XW_X)\) with \(C_X\) Hermitian and \(\mathbb Z_3\)-circulant, \(W_u=W_d=W_q\),
\(W_e=W_\nu=W_l\) from v4.9.0-r47, one common \(\varepsilon\ge0\), Dirac neutrinos.  Masses are the moduli of the eigenvalues; mixing is
\(U_u^\dagger U_d\) and \(U_e^\dagger U_\nu\).  Data fixed in the note: running masses at \(M_Z\) (Huang and Zhou 2021), neutrino
mass splittings and \(3\sigma\) ranges of NuFIT~6.0 (normal ordering), CKM magnitudes of the PDG 2024.  All solution branches are followed
from \(\varepsilon=0\) (24 assignments per sector) in steps of \(0.005\) up to \(\varepsilon=3\).

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r50 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r50-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r50.1 (numerical).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Solutions with the measured quark masses exist only up to \(\varepsilon\approx0.06\) (up) and \(0.125\) (down).  In none of the
\(7488\) combinations of \(\varepsilon\) and branch pair are \(|V_{us}|\), \(|V_{cb}|\), \(|V_{ub}|\) all within \(\pm20\%\); the closest case is
\(96\%\) off.
\item[(b)] Hence no common \(\varepsilon\) exists for quarks and leptons in the sense of H34b.  The leptons alone, with their own
\(\varepsilon\), reach the \(3\sigma\) ranges in no case either; the closest case is up to \(71\%\) outside.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Script \texttt{llm/k90\_test\_h34.py} with output \texttt{llm/k90\_out.txt}.  The first run stopped after H34a in a diagnostic print; only that
print and the treatment of \(m_1=0\) were corrected, and both outputs are kept.  The bound on \(\varepsilon\) was confirmed by random multistarts.
The lepton statement in (b) is a check after the evaluation.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H34a and H34b are refuted; H34c does not count.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The model assumption of v4.9.0-r47, the mask as a weak perturbation of circulant couplings, does not produce the measured mixing,
neither for quarks nor for leptons.  The qualitative reading of r47 (stronger lepton mixing) is withdrawn as a quantitative statement.
\item The reason is structural.  For a nearly rank-one coupling the term \(\varepsilon^2WCW\) creates a mass of order
\(\tfrac23\varepsilon^2m_t\) (quarks) or \(2\varepsilon^2m_\tau\) (leptons), so the mass hierarchy forces small \(\varepsilon\); at small
\(\varepsilon\) the democratic mask cannot produce \(|V_{us}|\gg|V_{cb}|\gg|V_{ub}|\) or the large PMNS angles.
\item Unaffected are v4.9.0-r47.1(a), (b), (d): the shape of the mask, its frame behaviour and its Fourier form.  Mixing needs another
form of coupling or a further breaking.
\end{enumerate}
