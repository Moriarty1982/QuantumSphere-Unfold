\paragraph{Purpose.}
After r54 the place with the widest reach is the branch Q1a of the cross-section Q1, the orientation (r55.6).  It has two halves: the sign of
the octant step, which r31.1 identified as a time orientation of the readout, and the counting of OP1, whose weight two requires a readout that
distinguishes the two orientations \(|j,\pm j\rangle\) of a puncture without selecting one (r53.2, r53.4).  Remark r31.2 already noted that a temporal
order of the loop makes the octant phase even under time reversal, as an identity.  This layer therefore asks what a temporally ordered
octant readout does on a puncture of spin \(j\): does it separate \(m=+j\) from \(m=-j\) with equal weight for every \(j\), and is the counting rule it
supplies compatible with the data?  Hypothesis H123 was fixed before the computation (commit \texttt{1a4662ef}); H123a--c are not blind,
H123d had a rough estimate beforehand.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r58 setting}
\label{subsec:v4100-r58-setting}

A puncture of spin \(j\in\{\tfrac12,1,\dots,20\}\) carries the frame maps \(R_j=e^{-i\pi J_z}\), \(T_j=e^{-i\pi J_x}\), \(J_{B,j}=e^{-i\pi J_y}\) (assumption (A) of r17.3).
An octant triple of spin \(j\) consists of the extremal states \(|a\rangle=|j,s_zj\rangle_z\), \(|b\rangle=|j,s_xj\rangle_x\), \(|c\rangle=|j,s_yj\rangle_y\), with
\(\varepsilon=s_xs_ys_z\).  Setting S123 (temporally ordered readout): three windows in the order \(R\to T\to J_B\) (\(\tau=+1\)) or reversed (\(\tau=-1\)); the
readout value is the Bargmann invariant \(B_\tau\), operationally the imaginary part of the Kirkwood--Dirac quasi-probability \(\operatorname{Tr}(P_cP_bP_a)\)
of three windows.  Time reversal of the protocol reverses the order and applies \(\Theta_j=e^{-i\pi J_y}K\).  The orientation of a puncture is
\(s_z\mapsto-s_z\) at fixed \(s_x,s_y\).  The origin of \(\tau\) is not derived (r31.2).

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r58 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r58-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r58.1 (H123a: the octant phase on spin \(j\); not blind).}
For every \(j\le20\), all eight triples and both \(\tau\),
\[
B_\tau=2^{-3j}\,e^{\,i\tau\varepsilon j\pi/2}.
\]
Reversing the order conjugates \(B\), \(\Theta_j\) conjugates \(B\), and both together leave it fixed.  This is r31.1 and r31.2 lifted to spin \(j\).
\emph{Proof.} Extremal states are spin-coherent states; the overlaps are the \(2j\)-th powers of the spin-\(\tfrac12\) overlaps, so \(B\) is the \(2j\)-th power of
\(2^{-3/2}e^{i\tau\varepsilon\pi/4}\) (r31.1).  Checked exactly in 640 cases (Wolfram), and as a quantum circuit (cycle test with an ancilla and a
controlled cyclic shift of three registers, Qiskit) for \(j=\tfrac12,1,2\): largest deviation \(6.6\cdot10^{-16}\), with \(10^5\) shots at most \(0.70\sigma\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r58.2 (H123b, H123c: which orientations are separated).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] The temporally ordered octant readout gives the two orientations equal modulus \(2^{-3j}\) for every \(j\) and values \(e^{\pm ij\pi/2}2^{-3j}\).
These coincide exactly for \(j\in\{2,4,6,\dots\}\), where \(B(+j)=B(-j)=(-1)^{j/2}2^{-3j}\).  The readout separates the orientations exactly for \(j\notin2\mathbb N\).
H123b (all \(j\) separated) is refuted, as expected from the backward search.
\item[(b)] The single-window readout, the eigenvalue \(e^{\mp i\pi j}\) of \(R_j\) on \(|j,\pm j\rangle_z\), separates exactly the half-integer \(j\) (H123c, confirmed).
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Proof.} \(e^{ij\pi/2}=e^{-ij\pi/2}\) iff \(j\pi\in2\pi\mathbb Z\); \(e^{-i\pi j}=e^{i\pi j}\) iff \(j\in\mathbb Z\).  Checked exactly for \(j\le20\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r58.3 (H123d: the counting rule of the octant readout misses the data).}
Count M with weight two where the readout separates the orientations and weight one where it merges them (r53.2), and keep T at \(j=\tfrac12\) with
weight two (separated by (a)).  The root of \(\sum_jg_je^{-2\pi\gamma\sqrt{j(j+1)}}=1\) (\(j_{\max}=4000\), remainder below \(10^{-2500}\)) is
\[
\gamma_M'=0.2341229,\qquad \gamma_L'=\gamma_M'/\gamma_T=1.837930,
\]
with \(\beta'=0.543379\), \(m_W'=80.0994\)\,GeV (\(-20.3\sigma\) from PDG 2025) and \(\Omega_M'=0.2953\) (\(-0.26\sigma\) from DESI DR2).  The lepton window
\([0.545,0.570]\) and the \(m_W\) window of H98 are missed; H123d does not carry.  Control: weight two for all \(j\) reproduces \(\gamma_M=0.2375330\) (r17.3).
\emph{Proof.} Numerical root with 40 digits (Wolfram); separation for \(20<j\le4000\) continued by r58.2(a).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r58.4 (reading).}
A temporally ordered octant readout distinguishes the orientation of a puncture without selecting one, but only for \(j\notin2\mathbb N\).  It does not supply
U2, and as a counting rule it is incompatible with the windows of H98.  The two halves of Q1a are therefore not the same datum in this form: the
readout sees the octant sign (spin \(\tfrac12\)), not the weight two at every \(j\).  Under S123 the octant sign is a relation \(\tau\varepsilon\) between the
orbit of the field and the time direction of the readout and is even under time reversal; where \(\tau\) comes from remains open.  A phase-sensitive
triangle readout that supplied U2 would need a solid angle \(\Omega\) with \(j\Omega\notin\pi\mathbb Z\) for every \(j\).  Found afterwards (side computation, no
criterion): the bent triangle of the residual bend, phase \(\pi/4-\gamma_M/2\approx\tfrac23\) per spin \(\tfrac12\), separates every \(j\le20\), but it is built from \(\gamma_M\);
a readout drawing U2 from it would be circular until the bend is derived independently.  The calculation touches \(\beta_{\rm sym}\) as a ratio of two
counts, which meets trigger 3 of the deferred idea I1 (r40.5: two ends with their own time orientation); I1 is named as a possible way out, not
pursued.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r58 frames}
\label{subsec:v4100-r58-frames}

antiunitary \(\Theta_j\) (complex conjugation up to a rotation), not the tachyon map \(z\mapsto iz\) (r31, r51, r81).  \(\gamma_M\) belongs to M and \(\gamma_T\) to T; they
are compared only through \(\gamma_L\) as in H98.
