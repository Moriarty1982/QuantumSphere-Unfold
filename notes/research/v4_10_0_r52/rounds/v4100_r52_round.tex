\paragraph{Purpose.}
OP1 rests on assigning different horizon counting rules to the frames M and T, which is equivalent to a frame-dependent area quantum,
\(\gamma_M/\gamma_T=\gamma_L=\cosh2\eta_{\rm sym}\).  A unitary boost leaves the area spectrum unchanged (Rovelli--Speziale), and the Holst duality rotation does not
map \(\gamma_M\) onto \(\gamma_T\) (r31.3, r31.4).  The remaining candidate named in the record is the non-unitary frame map \(J_B\), never tested on the
area operator.  At the author's request this layer records hypothesis H121, fixed before the computation; H121a and H121b are not blind, H121c
is blind in its value.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\label{subsec:v4100-r52-setting}

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r52 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r52-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r52.1 (H121a confirmed: \(J_B\) is an automorphism).}
\(J_B\) fixes \(L_x\) and \(K_x\) and maps \(L_y\mapsto iK_z\), \(L_z\mapsto-iK_y\), \(K_y\mapsto-iL_z\), \(K_z\mapsto iL_y\); the images obey the same commutation relations.
\emph{Proof.} Exact conjugation in the vector representation (script \texttt{k241}).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r52.2 (H121b confirmed: conjugation keeps the spectrum).}
For every operator \(O\) and every invertible \(U\), unitary or not, \(UOU^{-1}\) has the eigenvalues of \(O\), since \(Ov=\lambda v\) implies \((UOU^{-1})(Uv)=\lambda(Uv)\).  Checked for \(J^2\) and
\(J_z\) in the representations \((\tfrac12,0)\), \((\tfrac12,\tfrac12)\), \((1,0)\) to \(8.9\times10^{-16}\).
\emph{Proof.} As stated; numerical check in script \texttt{k241}.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r52.3 (H121c refuted: the induced Barbero--Immirzi parameter).}
If \(K=\gamma L\) holds before \(J_B\), then afterwards \(K'=\Gamma'L'\) with \(\Gamma'=\operatorname{diag}(\gamma,-1/\gamma,-1/\gamma)\): across the axis \(\gamma\) becomes \(-1/\gamma\), along it \(\gamma\) stays.  At \(\gamma_M\) this
gives \(-4.2099\) and \(0.2375\), which match neither \(\gamma_T\) (relative deviations \(32.05\) and \(0.865\)) nor, through \(\gamma_M/|\gamma'|\), the number \(\gamma_L\) (\(0.970\) and \(0.464\)).
\emph{Proof.} Linear substitution in the conjugated generators (script \texttt{k241}).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r52.4 (reading).}
Non-unitarity alone does not change an area spectrum; only a reading of the area in T through the transformed simplicity condition could, and then
\(J_B\) gives the known duality \(\gamma\leftrightarrow-1/\gamma\) across the axis and leaves \(\gamma\) along it, a direction-dependent change rather than one factor.  With the unitary boost
(Rovelli--Speziale), the Holst duality rotation (r31.3, r31.4) and now \(J_B\), the last structural candidate for a frame-dependent area quantum named in
the record does not carry.  OP1 therefore rests on the assignment of the counting rules themselves, U2 for M and the rule \(j=\tfrac12\) for T.  In the
finite representations checked here the complex boost is even unitary; in the unitary infinite-dimensional representations of the Lorentz group it
is not, and questions of domain remain open there.
