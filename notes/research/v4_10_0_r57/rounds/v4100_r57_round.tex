\subsection*{v4.10.0-r57 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r57-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r57.1 (the attractor fixes the rate).}
Exploration, Wolfram Language exact (\texttt{k243}, part A).  For the bridge of r05.5 (vacuum decays into matter at rate \(\beta^2\Gamma\), \(\Gamma=\varepsilon H\)), flat and
without radiation,
\[
\frac{d\Omega_M}{dN}=(1-\Omega_M)(\varepsilon\beta^2-3\Omega_M),\qquad N=\ln a .
\]
The fixed points are \(\Omega_M=1\) (matter era, repelling for \(\varepsilon\beta^2<3\)) and \(\Omega_M^*=\varepsilon\beta^2/3\) (attracting).  Hence \(\Omega_M^*=\beta^2\) exactly
for \(\varepsilon=3\), i.e.\ \(\Gamma=3H\), one cascade step per e-fold of volume.  On the attractor \(w_{\rm eff}=-1+\beta^2=-0.698\) and the coupling is
\(\delta=\varepsilon\beta^2=0.906\).  The orbit runs from the matter era to \(\beta^2\), \((\Omega_M-\beta^2)/(1-\Omega_M)\propto e^{-3(1-\beta^2)N}\); today \(\Omega_M>\beta^2\).  The
value \(\varepsilon=3\) follows from the demand ``attractor \(=\beta^2\)'', not from the structure.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r57.2 (H124: not excluded by BAO, with \(\Omega_M\) today \(\approx0.50\)).}
Pre-registered (commit \texttt{7b2b665}), not blind.  Model of r57.1 with \(\varepsilon=3\), radiation included, \(\Omega_{M0}\in(\beta^2,1)\) and \(hr_d\) free; the 13 DESI DR2
BAO values of H97; refuted if \(\chi^2_{\min}>24.72\) (\(p<0.01\), 11 degrees of freedom).  Validity holds (flat \(\Lambda\)CDM: \(\Omega_m=0.2970\), \(hr_d=101.56\) Mpc,
\(\chi^2=10.55\)).  Result: \(\chi^2_{\min}=18.42\), \(p=0.072\), at \(\Omega_{M0}=0.5006\), \(hr_d=95.49\) Mpc.  \textbf{Not excluded.}  Against flat \(\Lambda\)CDM with the same
number of free parameters the model is worse by \(\Delta\chi^2=7.87\).  \emph{Exploration afterwards:} with \(\Omega_{M0}\) free, BAO allows small \(\varepsilon\) as well as
\(\Lambda\)CDM (\(\varepsilon=1\): \(\chi^2=9.35\)); \(\chi^2\) rises only towards \(\varepsilon=3\).

\paragraph{Consequence.}
The cascade has an attractor \(\beta^2\) at one rate and is compatible with BAO alone, but the fit puts \(\Omega_M\) today near \(0.50\): the value \(\beta^2\) lies in the
future, so the attractor does not explain ``why now'' and the epoch remains an input (as in r56).  The absolute value of \(\Lambda\) is not fixed either
(r56.4).  CMB, supernovae and growth are not tested; a coupling of \(0.906\) is expected to be under strong pressure there (prior knowledge, not
computed).  OP5 is not closed; H96 stays open.
