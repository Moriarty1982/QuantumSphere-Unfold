\section*{v4.10.0-r43 Source-Only: Images of the Counterflow in the Family of Completed First Jets}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{v4.10.0-r43 Source-Only: Counterflow Images in the Completed Jet Family}
\label{sec:v4100-r43-jet-family}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Purpose.}
In v4.10.0-r42 no image of the co-moving blocks fits the counterflow block in the first derivatives of the stored seed jet.  The completed
conditions \(R=(F,G,dg)\) of the comparison model, however, have 32 components of which 31 are independent, against 96 real first derivatives at
fixed \(\psi\); the stored jet is one member of a family.  This layer asks whether the family contains jets in which the counterflow block is a
local image.  It is an exploration with a method fixed before the computation; it is partly not blind, since a count of equations suggested
that each image alone is admissible.  Earlier layers are not rewritten; Remark r43.3 corrects a reading of r42.2.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r43 setting}
\label{subsec:v4100-r43-setting}

The completed first-jet root of the seed differs from the jet of r42 by \(1.7\times10^{-14}\) and satisfies \(|R|=2\times10^{-40}\); the measures of r42 are
unchanged on it.  For an image \(X\in\{\mathrm T,\mathrm R,\mathrm I\}\) of r42 the 40 conditions \(m_X=\partial_\mu F_2-X(\partial_\mu F_0)=0\) are linear in the derivatives.
A damped Newton method of minimal norm (halving, at most 12 steps) solves \(R=0\) and \(m_X=0\) jointly; an image is admissible if \(|R|\le10^{-20}\) and
the relative image deviation is at most \(10^{-2}\).  The computation runs at 40 digits on the existing reconstruction of \(R\).

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r43 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r43-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r43.1 (the jet family).}
The Jacobian of \(R\) with respect to the 96 derivatives has rank 31 at the completed root, so the linearized family has dimension 65.  The stacked
matrices \([R;m_X]\) have full rank 71 for \(X=\mathrm T,\mathrm R,\mathrm I\); \([R;m_{\mathrm T};m_{\mathrm R}]\) has rank 79 of 111.
\emph{Proof.} Central differences at 40 digits and singular values (script \texttt{jet\_\allowbreak family\_\allowbreak images.py}).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r43.2 (time-reversed and reflected images are both admissible).}
For \(X=\mathrm T\) the method reaches \(|R|=1.7\times10^{-40}\) with image deviation \(7\times10^{-41}\) after 9 steps; for \(X=\mathrm R\) it reaches \(|R|=3.0\times10^{-40}\) with
image deviation \(3\times10^{-41}\) after 8 steps.  In both jets the derivatives change by amounts up to about 3.  For \(X=\mathrm I\) the method stops after
12 damped steps at \(|R|=0.15\), and for T and R together the line search makes no progress.
\emph{Proof.} As r43.1; the Newton history is in the output of run 3.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r43.3 (reading and correction of r42.2).}
Against the criteria fixed beforehand the result is \emph{underdetermined}: the family of completed first jets contains a jet in which the
counterflow block is exactly the time-reversed image of block 0 and another in which it is exactly the reflected image.  The first jet therefore
does not decide between time reversal and reflection, and the finding of r42 that no image fits describes the stored member of the family,
not the seed.  The restriction of the equal weighting to the seed point stated in r42.2 holds only in this sense.  That the internal reversal
and the simultaneous image were not reached is a finding of this procedure, not a proof of impossibility.  Higher orders or a regional
solution would be needed to decide.
