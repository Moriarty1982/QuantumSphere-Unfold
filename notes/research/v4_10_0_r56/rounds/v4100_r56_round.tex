\subsection*{v4.10.0-r56 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r56-results}

\paragraph{Definition v4.10.0-r56.1 (the class \(\mathcal K\)).}
\(\mathcal K\) consists of the constructions of the record that bear on a vacuum term:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(K1)] the frame maps M, T, P as passive invertible maps and the readouts built from them (dimensionless ratios such as \(\beta_{\rm sym}\), \(\gamma_L\),
\(\Omega_M=\beta^2\)) (r69, r05.4);
\item[(K2)] the reduced actions of W1/W2 under G1 and G2, with the weights \(a\), \(b\) of the two readings (v4.9.0-r84, r03.5, r04);
\item[(K3)] the semiclassical route from relative entropy (r06.1, r06.3);
\item[(K4)] the OP5 comparison model including its affine branch (r69);
\item[(K5)] the Markov cascade of v4.9.0-r31 and the bridges to cosmic time with \(\Gamma=\varepsilon H\) (r05.5).
\end{enumerate}
\(\mathcal K\) is a list of the record, not a class of all conceivable extensions.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r56.2 (the constant drops out of every equation of motion in \(\mathcal K\)).}
Exploration, analytic, checked with Wolfram Language (\texttt{k242}).
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] In (K2) the shift \(V\to V+c\) of the M reading \(\mathcal L=a(\tfrac12X-V)\) leaves the field equation unchanged (difference \(0\), exact) and adds
\((\rho,p)=(ac,-ac)\), \(w=-1\), to the source.  This is the free \(\Lambda=aV_{\min}\) of r03.5; under G1 the same holds with \(a+b\).
\item[(b)] Whenever the metric equations hold only in their trace-free part, as in (K3) (\(\Lambda\) enters through \(\nabla^a(\Lambda g_{ab})=0\), r06.3), the
Bianchi identity and \(\nabla^aT_{ab}=0\) give \(R+\kappa T=4\Lambda\) with \(\Lambda\) constant and arbitrary.  In flat FLRW the trace-free equation
\(\dot H=-\tfrac\kappa2(\rho+p)\) and \(\dot\rho=-3H(\rho+p)\) give \(\tfrac{d}{dt}(H^2-\kappa\rho/3)=0\) (exact); the constant is the integration constant.
\item[(c)] In (K1) a common energy offset is unobservable at fixed background, and a passive invertible frame change only rescales a balance defect
(r69).  In (K4) a branch solves the field equations for every constant (r69).  In (K5) the flux fractions are \(\beta^2\), \(1-\beta^2\) for every rate;
the bridge fixes the rate only by setting \(\varepsilon\) (refuted for \(\varepsilon=1\), H97; free for \(\varepsilon\lesssim0.4\)).
\end{enumerate}
No construction in \(\mathcal K\) has an equation that selects a value of the constant.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r56.3 (no scale in \(\mathcal K\)).}
Exploration.  All numbers that \(\mathcal K\) fixes are dimensionless and of order one (\(\beta_{\rm sym}^2=0.3018466\), \(\gamma_L=1.8647\), branching
fractions).  \(\Lambda\) has dimension length\({}^{-2}\).  \(\mathcal K\) contains no length of its own: W1/W2 fix no scale (r55.3), cone data fix neither an absolute
scale nor the weight of a vacuum term (r69), and the Planck length enters only through \(G\), from outside.  Measured in Planck units the value
would be \(\Lambda\ell_P^2=2.85\times10^{-122}\) (input: Planck 2018, \(H_0=67.4\) km\,s\(^{-1}\)Mpc\(^{-1}\), \(\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847\)), i.e.\ \(-\ln(\Lambda\ell_P^2)=279.9\).
No construction of \(\mathcal K\) supplies a dimensionless number of that size.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r56.4 (the readout trades \(\Lambda\) for the epoch).}
Exploration, checked with Wolfram Language.  With \(\rho_M\propto x^{-3}\) (\(x=a/a_0\)) and \(\rho_\Lambda=3\Lambda/\kappa\), the condition \(\Omega_M(x)=\beta^2\) has for every
\(\Lambda>0\) exactly one positive solution,
\[
x_*^3=\frac{\kappa\rho_{M0}(1-\beta^2)}{3\beta^2\Lambda},\qquad \Lambda=3(1-\beta^2)H_*^2=2.0944603\,H_*^2 .
\]
So the readout \(\Omega_M=\beta^2\) (H96) holds at one epoch for every value of \(\Lambda\); it does not fix \(\Lambda\).  It fixes \(\Lambda\) in units of the Hubble
rate at that epoch.  Of the pair (value of \(\Lambda\), epoch of the readout) exactly one datum is free.  The author's decision of 6 November 2026
(epoch ``today'', r05.3) supplies it.

\paragraph{Corollary v4.10.0-r56.5 (negative closure of the OP5 remainder, under \(\mathcal K\)).}
Within \(\mathcal K\) the absolute value of \(\Lambda\) is an input, as in general relativity and in unimodular gravity.  Equivalently, the epoch of
the readout is an input; H96 converts one into the other.  The statement is a no-go for the record, not for every extension: a construction
outside \(\mathcal K\) with its own length, or with a dimensionless number near \(10^{-122}\), would escape it.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{What is set:} the list \(\mathcal K\); \(\Lambda\) or, equivalently, the epoch ``today''.
\item \emph{What is derived:} that no equation of \(\mathcal K\) fixes the constant (r56.2), that \(\mathcal K\) has no scale (r56.3), and the exchange of
\(\Lambda\) and epoch under the readout (r56.4).
\item \emph{Where the open choice goes:} to the epoch.  The coincidence ``why now'' is the same input as the value of \(\Lambda\), not a second one.
\end{itemize}
OP5 is not closed by this layer.  The core closure stays local under the guards (r05.3(i)); the readout H96 stays open and is decided by data;
the attractor route (\(\Omega_M\to\beta^2\) as attractor, Q2) is deferred as idea I7 with triggers.
