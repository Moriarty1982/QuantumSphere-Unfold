# Layer v4.10.0-r09: the counting rule per frame, its singular position (H98) and a principle

After r08.3 the assignment of horizon counting rules to the frames (M all spins with weight 2, T only j = ½) is the most loaded assumption: H15, H46 and H96 rest on it.

| Record | Content |
|---|---|
| r09.1 | **H98 confirmed** (pre-registered, commit 0cad2d8; not blind with respect to the data). Rule space fixed beforehand: spin ranges all / half-integer / integer / only ½ / only 1, weights 2 / 2j+1; nine rules, 36 pairs. Pairs in the lepton window 2, in the m_W window (PDG 2025, 2σ) 1, in the Ω_M window (DESI DR2, 2σ) 2; all three only DL/M with ABCK. The SU(2) pair misses all three. A chance triple agreement is of the order of a few per cent; the m_W relation was found afterwards, so this is no strict significance. |
| r09.2 | **Principle P (exploration, not blind):** a frame counts the states that saturate the uncertainty bound (P1) and fit its energy condition (P2: M all levels, T at its floor only the smallest area; bridge: local first law E ∝ A). P selects exactly the confirmed pair. The counting rule is reduced to two readings with a reason of their own, not derived; the bridge in P2 (floor energy vs. horizon energy) is open. |
