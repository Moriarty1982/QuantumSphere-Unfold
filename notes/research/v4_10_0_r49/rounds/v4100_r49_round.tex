\subsection*{v4.10.0-r49 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r49-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r49.1 (R is fixed only up to boosts along its axis).}
A field with \(\mathbf E\parallel\mathbf B\) along \(z\) is invariant under boosts along \(z\) and changes under transverse boosts.  The symmetric tensors invariant under boosts along and
rotations about \(z\) are exactly \(S=\operatorname{diag}(s_1,s_2,s_2,-s_1)\); the energy-momentum tensor of the field, \(T=\rho\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-1)\), is one of them.  For every such
propagation tensor the speed along the axis is exactly 1; for \(G=\eta^{-1}+\lambda T\) the transverse squared speed is \((1-\lambda\rho)/(1+\lambda\rho)\), equal to \(\tfrac12\) at \(\lambda\rho=\tfrac13\).
\emph{Proof.} Exact Lorentz transformation and invariance under the generators (script \texttt{rest\_\allowbreak frame\_\allowbreak R.wl}).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r49.2 (the cone limit with a slowing from R).}
With \(r_0(\alpha)=r_m(\alpha)=1-\alpha/2\) and the values of r47, the largest squared characteristic speed is \(1.040\) at \(\alpha=0\) and stays above one at \(\alpha=\tfrac1{20}\) for
\(c_h^2=\tfrac12\), and is \(0.675\) at \(\alpha=1\).  Near the axis the massive channel couples (\(j_m^2=\tfrac23g_m^2\) at \(\alpha=0\)) while its speed is luminal; the massless channel
does not couple there (\(j_0=0\)).  For \(c_h^2=1\) the limit is violated at all grid points.
\emph{Proof.} As r47.1 with direction-dependent speeds.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r49.3 (no-go for the luminal Higgs radius; found afterwards).}
For \(c_h^2=1\) the upper condition of the cone limit is identically
\[
 A-\frac{j_0^2}{1-r_0}-\frac{j_m^2}{1-r_m}-b=-\tfrac23g_m^2(1+r_m-\alpha)-\frac{j_0^2}{1-r_0}-\frac{j_m^2}{1-r_m}<0
\]
for all \(r_0,r_m\in(0,1)\), \(\alpha\in[0,1]\) and \(g_m>0\).  With a luminal Higgs radius the directed coupling violates the metric cone limit for every choice of mediator speeds.
\emph{Proof.} Substitution of \(A\) and \(b\); all three terms are non-positive and the first is negative (script \texttt{higgs\_\allowbreak luminal\_\allowbreak check.py}, sympy).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r49.4 (reading).}
Against the criteria fixed beforehand the candidate R carries \emph{partially}: it can slow propagation only across its axis, and along the axis the
massive channel remains luminal and violates the limit.  Along the axis a frame is needed that breaks boosts along it, a timelike rest vector of a
medium, which a field with \(\mathbf E\parallel\mathbf B\) does not have.  The finding r49.3 concerns the whole comparison model: besides slow mediators the cone limit
requires a slowed Higgs radius (\(c_h^2<1\), set to \(\tfrac12\) in the model), whereas W2 forces \(c_h^2=1\) (r33).  The identification of the comparison field with
the radial mode of W2 (r33) and the directed coupling are therefore not causally compatible with any mediator, on either side of the fork of r45.
Three propagations would have to be slowed, and all three need a medium with its own rest vector.
