\subsection*{v4.9.0-r52 the two forms}
\label{subsec:v490-r52-forms}

With \(D_X=\exp(\varepsilon W_X)\), \(C_X\) Hermitian and \(\mathbb Z_3\)-circulant, \(W_u=W_d=W_q\), \(W_e=W_\nu=W_l\) and one common
\(\varepsilon\):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{form K} (congruence; the partner read through the time reversal as in v4.9.0-r47): \(M_X=D_XC_XD_X\), whose first order is the form of
r47 and which keeps the rank of \(C_X\);
\item \emph{form S} (similarity; the partner read through the tachyon map, which keeps its sector by v4.9.0-r51.1(a) and so carries the weight
\(-W\)): \(M_X=D_XC_XD_X^{-1}\).
\end{itemize}
Masses are singular values, mixing comes from the left singular vectors.  Data and procedure as in v4.9.0-r50, with step \(0.01\) up to
\(\varepsilon=6\).  The note discloses that form K is a Froggatt--Nielsen texture with charges \(w=(0,2,1)\) and \(\lambda=e^{-\varepsilon}\), for
which \(|V_{cb}|\sim|V_{us}|\) was expected.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r52 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r52-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r52.1 (numerical).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Form K: solutions with the measured masses exist up to \(\varepsilon\approx3.5\) (up) and \(2.3\) (down); in none of \(98\,064\)
combinations are \(|V_{us}|\), \(|V_{cb}|\), \(|V_{ub}|\) all within \(\pm20\%\).  The closest case is \(36\%\) off
(\(\varepsilon=1.26\): \(0.144\), \(0.036\), \(0.0050\)).
\item[(b)] Form S: likewise none of \(102\,528\); the closest case is \(40\%\) off (\(\varepsilon=1.32\): \(0.135\), \(0.035\), \(0.0051\)).
\item[(c)] Hence no common \(\varepsilon\) for quarks and leptons in either form.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Script \texttt{llm/k92\_test\_h36.py}, output \texttt{llm/k92\_out.txt}; a count in the check after the evaluation was added after the first run
(\texttt{k92\_out\_erstlauf.txt}), the criterion values of both runs agree.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H36a--d are refuted.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Neither exact form produces the measured mixing with one \(\varepsilon\) and circulant couplings.  Both are much closer than the linear
perturbation of v4.9.0-r50 (\(36\%\) instead of \(96\%\) for the closest quark case), and the ordering
\(|V_{us}|\gg|V_{cb}|\gg|V_{ub}|\) comes out; the hierarchy no longer bounds \(\varepsilon\) to \(\lesssim0.06\).
\item A check after the evaluation, not part of the criterion: the leptons alone, with their own \(\varepsilon\), reach the \(3\sigma\) ranges in
form K never and in form S in \(12\) of \(125\,922\) combinations (\(\varepsilon\approx0.5\), \(m_1=0.003\) eV, \(\sin^2\theta_{23}\) at the
edge).  With this many branches that is a chance hit, not a finding.
\item What is missing is, by the criterion, an open choice and not a result: couplings other than circulant, or a separate \(\varepsilon\) for each
sector.  With one \(\varepsilon\) per sector the number of free parameters would match the number of mixing quantities, and that would no longer
be an explanation.
\end{enumerate}
