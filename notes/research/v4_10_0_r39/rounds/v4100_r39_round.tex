\paragraph{Purpose.}
The field ansatz of v4.10.0-r38 reproduces the mediator term of the OP5 comparison model only with a scalar whose weight \(g_s^2/g^2=\tfrac12\) and
whose degeneracy with the tensor are set.  This layer asks whether both follow when the scalar is a component of one symmetric tensor field on
the subspace orthogonal to the axis.  It is an exploration with a method fixed before the computation and is not blind; the distinguished case
was sketched by hand when the method was written.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r39 ansatz}
\label{subsec:v4100-r39-ansatz}

Let \(H_{ab}\), \(a,b\in\{0,1,2\}\), be symmetric on the subspace \((t,x,y)\) orthogonal to the axis, with one mass \(m\), one speed \(r_m\) and positive
kinetic energy for every component, \(\mathcal L_H=\tfrac12\sum_{ab}[(\partial_tH_{ab})^2-r_m(\nabla H_{ab})^2-m^2H_{ab}^2]\), coupled by \(g'H^{ab}J_{ab}\) with
\[
 J_{ab}=\partial_a\partial_b\eta-c\,\eta_{ab}\,\Box_\parallel\eta,\qquad \eta_{ab}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1),\qquad \Box_\parallel=\partial_t^2-\partial_x^2-\partial_y^2,
\]
and a weight \(w\ge0\) with which the time--transverse components \(H_{0i}\) enter the coupling.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r39 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r39-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r39.1 (exchange).}
The exchange numerator is
\[
 N(c,w)=(3c^2-2c+1)(x^2+a_\perp^2)+(4c-6c^2+2w)\,a_\perp x ,
\]
and \(N=\lambda N_m\) identically exactly for \(w=\tfrac12(3c+1)(c-1)\), \(\lambda=\tfrac32(3c^2-2c+1)\).  For \(w=1\) this requires \(c=(1\pm\sqrt{10})/3\); for
\(w=0\) it gives \(c=1\) or \(c=-\tfrac13\), both with \(\lambda=3\), that is \(g'^2=g_m^2/3\).
\emph{Proof.} Plane waves, \(\partial_a\partial_b\to D_aD_b\) with \(D=(-i\omega,ik_1,ik_2)\); sum of squared vertices over ordered index pairs, coefficient
comparison.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r39.2 (conservation selects \(c=1\)).}
\(p^aJ_{ab}=0\) identically exactly for \(c=1\); then the matching of r39.1 requires \(w=0\).  At \(c=1\) the vertices are \(-a_\perp\) for \(H_{00}\) and
\(-k_ik_j-(x-a_\perp)\delta_{ij}\) for \(H_{ij}\).  The coupling is free of ghosts for \(g_m^2<3\), the static numerator is \(\tfrac23g_m^2a_\perp^2\) as in the model, so
the static bound \(g_m^2\le3r_m\) of r38.2 holds unchanged, and the dispersion grid of r38 gives the same roots.
\emph{Proof.} \(p^aJ_{ab}=(c-1)\,p_b\,p^2\); the time derivatives enter only through the trace of \(H_{ij}\); the effective \(\eta\) operator equals that of r38.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r39.3 (reading).}
One field with one mass now supplies the weight and the degeneracy of the scalar, and the distinguished coupling is the identically conserved
combination \(J_{ab}\), the version on the subspace orthogonal to the axis of the improvement \(S^{\mu\nu}=p^\mu p^\nu-\eta^{\mu\nu}d\) from which r32.0
obtained the form of the comparison operator.  Against the criteria fixed beforehand the result carries \emph{partially}: the decoupling of
\(H_{0i}\) (\(w=0\)) is a structural choice; in a Fierz--Pauli-like dynamics the time components would be auxiliary fields without a pole of their own,
which is a reading.  The time--time component couples to \(\Delta_\perp\eta\), not to \(\partial_t^2\eta\), and the decomposition differs from r38: the pair
(\(H_{00}\), trace) carries \(\tfrac12a_\perp^2-\tfrac23a_\perp x+\tfrac23x^2\) and the traceless part \(\tfrac16a_\perp^2\), against \(\tfrac13a_\perp^2-\tfrac23a_\perp x+\tfrac23x^2\) and \(\tfrac13a_\perp^2\) in r38,
with the same sum and no rotation in field space between them.  The transverse vertex of the single field is not the string vertex
\(k_ik_j-\tfrac12x\,\delta_{ij}\) of r37; whether the string carries only part of the mediator or the single field is not its field version is open.
