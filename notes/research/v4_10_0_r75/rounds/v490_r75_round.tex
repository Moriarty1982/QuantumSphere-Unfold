\section*{v4.9.0-r75 Source-Only: Renaming the Millennium Matrix to Compensator Matrix}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{v4.9.0-r75 Source-Only: Renaming the Millennium Matrix to Compensator Matrix}
\label{sec:v490-r75-compensator-matrix}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Purpose.}
The \(16\times16\) matrix \(\mathcal M_{16}\) of \S\ref{sec:millennium-hc} was called the Millennium matrix.  The name suggested
a link to the Millennium Prize Problems that the Companion does not claim, and the author had asked several times for a name
that says what the object does.  On 3 November 2026 the author decided: \(\mathcal M_{16}\) is called the \emph{compensator
matrix}, because it carries several Heisenberg compensators and extends the local rule \(C=\tfrac12(\mathrm{id}+J)\) to all
layers (\S\ref{sec:millennium-hc}; in v4.9.0-r42 one of its compensators acts as chirality projector).  This layer records the
renaming.  No mathematical statement changes.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\noindent\textbf{Source-only status.}
This v4.9.0-r75 layer is source-only.  It does not build a PDF, does not assign a new DOI,
and does not authorize a public release.  The v4.9.0 Version DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23534954}{10.5281/zenodo.23534954} and the Concept DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19210728}{10.5281/zenodo.19210728} are unchanged by it.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r75 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r75-results}

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r75.1 (naming rule).}
From this layer on, \(\mathcal M_{16}\) is the \emph{compensator matrix}.  Unchanged are the symbol \(\mathcal M_{16}\) (and
\(\mathcal M\), \(\mathcal M^\mu{}_\nu\) where used), every label (for example \texttt{sec:millennium-hc}), and the
abbreviation MM in MM/HC, MM split and similar compounds, which is kept as a historical abbreviation for the matrix
\(\mathcal M_{16}\).  Wherever an earlier layer, an inherited appendix or a note says ``Millennium matrix'', read
``compensator matrix''.

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r75.2 (where the name was changed).}
The main body uses the new name in the nomenclature, in the sections on the probe--closure field system, the structural core,
the HC hypotheses and the master model, including the section title of \S\ref{sec:millennium-hc} and the definition, which
names the former term once.  On the research line the 4.8 sections on the native operator link and the coupling candidate are
changed likewise, and the PDF keywords.  In the local monolith the 27 main-body occurrences are changed by the patch list
\texttt{llm/revision\_spec\_\allowbreak v490\_\allowbreak r75.py}; the main body is again word-identical with the research
line.  Inherited appendices (repair, transport and OP protocols, technical tools, release records), the archive and all earlier
layers keep the old wording verbatim.  The bibliography entry ``Set Theory: The Third Millennium Edition'' is a title and not
affected.

\paragraph{Consequence.}
A name change only.  No definition, theorem, proof, number, register entry or guard is changed.
