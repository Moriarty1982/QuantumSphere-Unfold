\section*{v4.10.0-r44 Source-Only: Second-Jet Extension of the Counterflow Image Jets}

\paragraph{Purpose.}
By v4.10.0-r43 the family of completed first jets of the OP5 seed contains a jet in which the counterflow block is the time-reversed image of
block 0 and one in which it is the reflected image.  A first jet is admissible only if it extends to a second jet: the prolonged system has 128
equations in 240 second-order coefficients and rank 113, so 15 compatibility conditions act on the first jet.  This layer tests whether they
separate the two image jets.  It is an exploration with a method fixed before the computation, including the check of the search direction
required after several variants with the same open premise; it is blind in its outcome.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r44 setting}
\label{subsec:v4100-r44-setting}

For a first jet \(v\) with \(R(v)=0\) the symbol \(J(v)\) and the right-hand side \(b(v)\) (total derivative of \(R\) with vanishing second jet) are
recomputed, the \(128\times240\) system \(A(v)c+b(v)=0\) is assembled as in the existing second-jet script, and the smallest full residual over
\(c\) is determined.  A first jet extends if this residual is at most \(10^{-20}\).  Tested are the stored completed jet, the time-reversed and the
reflected image jets of r43 (regenerated by the same Newton method), and two random members of the family (unit step along a random direction
of the null space with fixed seeds, followed by Newton back to \(R=0\)).  The computation runs at 40 digits.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r44 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r44-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r44.1 (all tested jets extend).}
At all five jets the prolonged system has rank 113 of 128, and the smallest full residuals are \(1.5\times10^{-21}\) (stored jet),
\(6.6\times10^{-23}\) (time-reversed image), \(5.3\times10^{-22}\) (reflected image), \(2.4\times10^{-23}\) and \(3.7\times10^{-21}\) (random members), against
right-hand sides of size 27 to 38.  The image jets lie at distances \(3.16\) and \(2.67\) from the stored jet, the random members at about \(0.98\).
\emph{Proof.} Numerical evaluation at 40 digits (script \texttt{second\_\allowbreak jet\_\allowbreak images.py}); the residual level \(10^{-21}\) of the control is set by
central differences for the symbol and numerical differentiation for \(b\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r44.2 (reading).}
Against the criteria fixed beforehand the result is \emph{no restriction}: the compatibility conditions of second order hold on all tested
members of the first-jet family, the random members included, and therefore do not separate time reversal from reflection.  Five members are
not a proof that the conditions are identities on the whole family.  With the values at the seed (r41), the first jet (r43) and the second jet
(this layer) none of the available levels decides the question; higher jets depend on the open Omega condition and the unresolved degree-five
cohomology of the seed program, and no regional solution exists.  The deferred idea I1 (a string between M and T) remains the other route; it
is put to the author and not pursued silently.
