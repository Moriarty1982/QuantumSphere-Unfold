\subsection*{v4.10.0-r47 setting}
\label{subsec:v4100-r47-setting}

In the reduced comparison model the principal part of the coupled system of the Higgs radius and the two mediators is \(K=\operatorname{diag}(A,1,1)\) and
\[
 V=\begin{pmatrix} b & j_0 & j_m\\ j_0 & r_0 & 0\\ j_m & 0 & r_m\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A=Z-\tfrac23g_m^2,\quad b=Zc_h^2+\tfrac23g_m^2(r_m-\alpha),\quad j_0^2=\tfrac12g_0^2\alpha^2,\quad j_m^2=\tfrac23g_m^2(r_m^2-r_m\alpha+\alpha^2),
\]
with \(\alpha=a_\perp/q\in[0,1]\).  The squared characteristic speeds are the eigenvalues of \(K^{-1/2}VK^{-1/2}\); they lie in \((0,1]\) exactly if \(V\succ0\) and \(K-V\succeq0\).  If
\(r_i=1\), the principal minor of \(K-V\) in channel \(i\) is \(-j_i^2\), so the coupling \(j_i\) must vanish.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r47 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r47-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r47.1 (luminal mediators violate the cone limit).}
With \(Z=1\) and \(g_0^2=g_m^2=\tfrac3{100}\) the largest squared characteristic speed over \(\alpha\) is \(0.675\) for the set values \(c_h^2=r_0=r_m=\tfrac12\), but \(1.064\) for
\(r_0=r_m=1\), \(1.199\) for \(r_0=r_m=1\) with \(c_h^2=1\) (as forced by W2, r33), \(1.031\) for \(r_0=1\) alone and \(1.041\) for \(r_m=1\) alone.  At \(r_m=1\),
\(j_m^2=\tfrac23g_m^2(1-\alpha+\alpha^2)\ge\tfrac12g_m^2>0\) for all \(\alpha\).
\emph{Proof.} Numerical eigenvalues at 30 digits on a grid in \(\alpha\) and the minor argument (script \texttt{cone\_\allowbreak check.wl}).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r47.2 (correction of r46.2 and r46.4).}
Theoremlet r46.2 is right that the leading static contrast does not depend on \(r_m\), but its statement that \(r_m=1\) is admissible used only the conditions of
r38.2 and missed the cone limit.  Both mediators of the reduced model must propagate more slowly than the metric speed; the slowed propagation
therefore remains a set input.  Against the strict criterion of r45 the readout branch carries only \emph{partially}: frame, axis and time
orientation of the coupling belong to the readout, the slowing does not.  A Lorentz-covariant field propagates luminally in every frame, so a cone
narrower than the metric one needs a distinguished frame on the field side.  The fork of r45.4 thus does not resolve to one side: the readout side
carries the frame of the coupling, the field side has to carry the slowing.

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r47.3 (consequences).}
(a) Already the massless channel with \(r_0=1\) violates the limit: in this reduced model the directed readout coupling of the Higgs radius is not
compatible with ordinary, luminal gravity.  (b) A bigravity of M and T on proportional backgrounds has two luminal spin-2 states and fails the
same condition; only a second cone narrower than the metric one could help, whereas H78 gives T a wider cone.  The route chosen by the author is
therefore closed in this form before a calculation of its own.
