\subsection*{v4.10.0-r18 status notes}
\label{subsec:v4100-r18-status}

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r18.1 (no blind test from r15 to r17).}
A consequence of r15 to r17 that fixes a quantity not yet measured, without a new free choice, was sought.  The observables already
covered by open predictions (\(\theta_{12}\) H9, \(m_W\) H15, \(\theta_{23}\) H42 and H46, \(\Omega_M\) H96, \(R\) H100, \(m_\tau\) H102, ordering and
\(\sum m_\nu\) H2) were set aside.  Checked candidates: neutrinos as readouts of one object with positive weights (r16.9, r17.2) cannot
reach \(K=\tfrac23\) (at most \(0.585\) for normal and \(0.500\) for inverted ordering; known from H2); ``Majorana means equal helicity parts''
gives \(R=0\) and ``one helicity only'' gives \(R=0.0052\), both against the observed \(0.0298\); \(\delta_{CP}\) from the sign of the Koide phase,
Majorana against Dirac from r16.5, and the strand statistics of r17.5 have no construction without a new choice.  No blind test was
found; H105 stays unassigned.  A blind test needs a structure that fixes the overlap \(\tfrac12\), the bend or the statistics per frame.

\paragraph{Remark v4.10.0-r18.2 (H2 recomputed; a correction).}
The unchanged script of H2 (v4.8.0-r12, pre-registration \texttt{434c5a8}) reproduces the result of 20 October 2026: normal ordering,
\(\sum m_\nu=0.05916\pm0.00023\) eV, lightest root negative, \(K=\tfrac23\); inverted ordering \(0.102\) eV.  With JUNO 2026
(\(\Delta m^2_{21}=7.50\pm0.12\cdot10^{-5}\) eV\(^2\)), SNO+ and the full NuFIT 6.1 table, \(\sum m_\nu\) stays \(0.0592\) eV and \(m_1\approx0.37\) meV; under the
fixed criterion H2 remains compatible and weakly discriminating, \(0.4\) meV above the floor of normal ordering.  The wiki had listed H2
with ``normal ordering supported by JUNO'' without a source; this is corrected: JUNO alone does not yet determine the ordering, while
a global analysis including the public JUNO data prefers normal ordering weakly (\(\Delta\chi^2=4.6\), arXiv:2601.09791).

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r18.3 (the Dirac readout cycle as the time cycle).}
The slot cycle \(\tau\), under which the Dirac couplings are circulant (v4.9.0-r55), is diagonalised by the Fourier matrix with eigenvalues
\(1,\omega_3,\bar\omega_3\), those of translation by one third of the period of \(s(t)\) (r16.9).  Under this correspondence the slot states are
the three equally weighted readouts at times \(0,T/3,2T/3\) and the Fourier modes are the mass eigenstates of the charged leptons; TM2
reads: one neutrino mass eigenstate is the equally weighted readout at one time.  The frame map \(T\) fixes every slot state; the
transposition \(\rho\) gives a column with \(|U_{e i}|^2=0\), \(\theta_{13}=0\) (cf.\ H6).

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r18.4 (the neutrino \(\mathbb Z_2\) as the centre of \(\mathrm{Spin}(8)\)).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] In \(\mathrm{Cl}(8)\) the rotation by \(2\pi\) is \(+1\) on the vector and \(-1\) on both chiral spinors; on the triality slots
\((8_v,8_s,8_c)\) it is the sign triple \((1,-1,-1)\) of v4.9.0-r38.1(c), the \(\mathbb Z_2\) that generates TM2 in r55.
\item[(b)] A symmetric Majorana matrix with \(DMD=M\), \(D=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,-1)\), decouples the vector slot; its fixed column in the flavour
basis is exactly \((\tfrac13,\tfrac13,\tfrac13)\) (500 random matrices, deviation \(10^{-16}\)).
\item[(c)] With the mask of v4.9.0-r57 (variant A of H41, same data and grid) only the \(\mathbb Z_2\) whose fixed slot has mask weight \(0\) give hits:
slots \(1\) and \(2\) with \(388\) each, together the \(776\) of H41, and the mask slot \(3\) none.  This split is evaluated after the fact.
\end{enumerate}
Reading: the group \(G_M\) left free in r55 is the fermion parity of \(\mathrm{Spin}(8)\) in the frame in which a mask-free slot is the vector;
the Majorana coupling respects it, the Dirac coupling respects \(\tau\).  By r47 the reference slot has mask weight \(0\), so ``fixed slot =
vector slot = reference slot'' is compatible with H41.  This is a reason for the pattern, not a new number: TM2 with the mask correction,
\(\theta_{23}\) free.  Why Majorana respects \((-1)^F\) and Dirac does not, the assignment of generations to slots (r37) and the choice of the
vector slot are assumed.
