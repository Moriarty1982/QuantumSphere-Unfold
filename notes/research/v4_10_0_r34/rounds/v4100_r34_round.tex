After v4.10.0-r33 the directed contrast of the OP5 case rests on a massive, subluminal mediator coupled linearly to the Higgs radius.  This
layer checks a fixed list of eight candidates from the Companion against five requirements: (V1) massive, (V2) subluminal, (V3) linear

fixed before the computation and is not blind.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.10.0-r34 results}
\label{subsec:v4100-r34-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r34.1 (the connectors of k185).}
With matter \(p_M=m(\cosh\eta,\sinh\eta)\) and tachyon image \(p_T=m(\sinh\eta,\pm\cosh\eta)\) at a common rapidity, the counter-propagating pair
connector \(q=p_M+p_T\) has
\[
 q^2=2m^2\sinh2\eta>0,\qquad \frac{q^1}{q^0}=-e^{-2\eta},
\]
so it is massive, subluminal (\(|v|=0.291\) at \(\eta_{\rm sym}\)) and tied to the axis of counter-propagation.  The counter-propagating
transition connector has \(q^2=-2m^2\sinh2\eta\) and \(|v|=e^{2\eta}\); the co-moving connectors are light-like.  \emph{Proof.} Direct
evaluation of \(q^2=(q^0)^2-(q^1)^2\) and \(q^1/q^0\).  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.10.0-r34.2 (the optical mode of the two-component material).}
For the two superposed continua of r32.2 with pair spring \(k\), the longitudinal optical branch has
\[
 \omega_0^2=\frac{k(\rho_1+\rho_2)}{\rho_1\rho_2},\qquad
 v_{\rm opt}^2=\frac{K(c_\sigma\rho_1^2+\rho_2^2)}{\rho_1\rho_2(\rho_1+\rho_2)},
\]
with resting centre of mass, \(\rho_1u+\rho_2w=0\).  The Higgs source \(\delta\mathcal J_h=Y_1\partial u+Y_2\partial w\), \(Y_a=\sum y\,n_0\) of component \(a\),
couples to this mode exactly if \(Y_1/\rho_1\neq Y_2/\rho_2\).  \emph{Proof.} The determinant of the plane-wave matrix gives the two
branches; at \(q=0\) the optical eigenvector is \((-\rho_2/\rho_1,1)\).  \(\square\)
