in the note.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r47 model assumption}
\label{subsec:v490-r47-assumption}

The exact chirality is that of Model~I.  The deviation \(\Delta=J_{\rm II}-J_{\rm I}\), on slot \(i\) given by \(\Delta_i=i(B_i-L_{e_7})\) with
\(B=(L_{e_7},R_{e_7},-L_{e_7}-R_{e_7})\), acts as a weak perturbation of the couplings with small weight \(\varepsilon\): a slot coupling \(M\) becomes
\(M+\varepsilon(WM+MW)\), with \(W\) the diagonal of slot weights of the sector, and the right-handed partner is read in the T frame
(v4.9.0-r41.1(c)).  This assumption is not derived.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r47 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r47-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r47.1.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] \(\Delta_i\) is scalar on each \(Q\)-sector, with slot weights \((0,0,-3)\) on sector \(0\), \((0,2,1)\) on \(\tfrac13\), \((0,-2,-1)\) on
\(\tfrac23\), \((0,0,3)\) on \(1\).
\item[(b)] \(T\Delta T^{-1}=-\Delta\); the weights are antisymmetric under \(q\leftrightarrow1-q\), so the partner read in the T frame carries the same
weights as the left-handed particle: \(W_q=\mathrm{diag}(0,-2,-1)\) for up and down quarks, \(W_l=\mathrm{diag}(0,0,-3)\) for leptons.
\item[(c)] \(W_q\) and \(W_l\) do not commute with \(\tau\); with \(\mathbb Z_3\)-circulant Hermitian \(M_u\), \(M_d\) and the same mask \(W_q\) the mixing
is non-zero for \(\varepsilon>0\) in all \(200\) random cases.  It is linear in \(\varepsilon\) in \(196\) of them at the pre-registered values
\(\varepsilon=10^{-3},10^{-4}\).
\item[(d)] In the Fourier basis of the generations all off-diagonal entries have equal size, \(1/\sqrt3\) for \(W_q\) and \(1\) for \(W_l\); the
ratio is \(\sqrt3\).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
(a), (b) from \(iL_{e_7},iR_{e_7}=\pm1\) on the sectors (v4.9.0-r43) and checked by matrices; (c), (d) numerically (script
\texttt{llm/k88\_test\_h32.py}).  A check after the evaluation found the four non-linear cases nearly degenerate (relative gap at most
\(3\cdot10^{-3}\), median \(7\cdot10^{-2}\)); at \(\varepsilon=10^{-6},10^{-7}\) all \(200\) are linear.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H32a, b and d are confirmed.  H32c is refuted as stated, since linearity fails in four of \(200\) cases at the pre-registered \(\varepsilon\); the
mixing is non-zero in all cases, and linearity holds in the perturbative range \(\varepsilon\ll\) gap.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Under the model assumption the generation mask comes from the structure.  The triality-covariant chirality is forbidden as an exact
symmetry, but its deviation from equal chirality is a fixed number per slot on every sector.  The shape of the mask is fixed,
\(\mathrm{diag}(0,-2,-1)\) for quarks and \(\mathrm{diag}(0,0,-3)\) for leptons; only \(\varepsilon\) is chosen.  Up and down share the mask, and
mixing still arises because their couplings differ (v4.9.0-r46).
\item In the generation basis the mask is democratic, with all pairs coupled equally, and \(\sqrt3\) stronger for leptons than for quarks.  At
equal \(\varepsilon\) this means stronger lepton than quark mixing, enhanced further by the close neutrino masses.  Qualitatively this is the
direction of observation; no numbers follow.
\item On slot~1 the weight is always zero, since both chiralities agree there.  Which slot is the reference is a choice of the kind met in
v4.9.0-r38.  In this picture the breaking of triality comes from the choice of a reference slot.
\item The model assumption, \(\varepsilon\), the circulant parameters and hence all numbers remain open.
\end{enumerate}
