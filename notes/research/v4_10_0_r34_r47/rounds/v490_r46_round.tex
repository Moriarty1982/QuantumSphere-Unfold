\subsection*{v4.9.0-r46 setting}
\label{subsec:v490-r46-setting}

On the slot space \(\mathbb C^3\) let \(P=\tau\), and let \(M_u\), \(M_d\) be Hermitian \(\mathbb Z_3\)-circulants (\(\rho\)-broken, \(\tau\)-symmetric, as
after v4.9.0-r45); the mixing is \(|V|=|U_u^\dagger U_d|\).  A \emph{shift readout} is built from the level readouts \(P^{2^j}\) of v4.9.0-r39:
products (reading \(\Sigma\)), averages over generated subgroups (reading \(\vee\)), and selector contractions \(\sum\chi_{mn}P^{2^m}P^{2^n}\).  A
\emph{kind mask}, with the readout kind taken to be the slot, is a diagonal projector \(D_S\), \(\emptyset\neq S\subsetneq\{1,2,3\}\), entering as
\(M+\delta D_S\).

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r46 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r46-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r46.1.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Every shift readout commutes with \(\tau\); added to \(M_u\) and \(M_d\) it leaves \(|V|\) a permutation matrix.
\item[(b)] Kind masks do not commute with \(\tau\); for \(\delta>0\) and generic \(M_u,M_d\), \(|V|\) is not a permutation matrix, even with the same
mask and weight for up and down.
\item[(c)] For small \(\delta\) the deviation of \(|V|\) from a permutation is linear in \(\delta\) and bounded by
\(\tfrac{\delta}{3}\bigl(\mathrm{gap}_u^{-1}+\mathrm{gap}_d^{-1}\bigr)\), with the smallest spacings of the eigenvalues.
\item[(d)] Kind masks are real and commute with the frame change \(T\).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
(a): shift readouts lie in the group ring \(\mathbb C[\mathbb Z_3]\), which is commutative, so they are circulant; circulants are diagonalized by
one Fourier matrix.  (b)--(d) checked on \(200\) random cases each (script \texttt{llm/k87\_test\_h31.py}); the factor \(\tfrac13\) in (c) is the
size of the entries of a one-slot mask in the Fourier basis.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H31a--d are confirmed; (a) and (c) are elementary.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The mixed readouts as they are formed from the Hilbert shift produce no mixing: every readout built only from the generation shifts
commutes with \(\tau\).  This is a genuine prohibition.
\item Only a readout that distinguishes generations as readout kinds breaks \(\tau\).  Then mixing arises, already with the same mask for up and
down, and it is small when the mask is weak and the masses are far apart.  This agrees qualitatively with small quark mixing at a strong mass
hierarchy and with larger mixing where masses lie close, as for neutrinos.  No numbers follow.
\item Mixing is measurable and not an artefact of an observer.  A mixing from readout can therefore only mean that the generation-selective
mask is part of the couplings, of the structure in which one reads out, not a dependence on who measures.  Where such a mask comes from in the
structure is open.
\end{enumerate}
