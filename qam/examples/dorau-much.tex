\section*{Change of physical readout for the same observer}
\qammap{area-assumption}{Rindler}{Geometry}
\[
\qamphysconvert{area-law}{conditional}{area-assumption}{boost-flux}{geometry}
  {\delta A=4S_H=8\pi J_B}
\]
\begin{qamcontext}{geometry}
\[
\qamformula{focusing}{conditional}
  {\delta A\simeq-\!\int_H U R_{UU}\,dU\,d\mathrm{vol}_{\mathcal S}}
\]
\[
\qamformula[area-law,focusing]{semiclassical-einstein}{conditional}
  {G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=8\pi\langle{:T_{ab}:}\rangle_{\omega_\phi}}
\]
\end{qamcontext}
The last step uses local matching in every null direction, conservation
and the contracted Bianchi identity. The area map is an imported
assumption; the wedge map is a restriction, not an inverse observer map.
See Dorau and Much, Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 091602 (2026), Eqs. (4),
(10)--(13), (18), (20)--(28), DOI 10.1103/lmq8-nsty.
