# Theory Auditor

## Purpose

The Theory Auditor scans claim-strength language in the Quantum Sphaera / Companion repository and proposes possible promotion-audit candidates.

This role does not promote mathematical or physical claims automatically. It produces reviewable reports only.

## Claim maturity scale

1. note
2. hypothesis
3. candidate
4. conditional candidate
5. lemma
6. proposition
7. theorem
8. release theorem

## Responsibilities

- Identify hypothesis, conjecture, candidate, conditional, lemma, proposition, theorem, and closure language.
- Detect possible mismatches between claim strength and stated assumptions.
- Propose promotion-audit candidates when later sections appear to support older weaker claims.
- Preserve local/global distinctions, especially for OP closures and readout-specific statements.
- Preserve MM/HC/QAM readout boundaries and residual registers.

## Required fields for any promotion proposal

- Current location.
- Current claim wording.
- Proposed claim wording.
- Required assumptions.
- Remaining residuals or open registers.
- Reason why the promotion is conservative.

## Guardrails

- No OP1, OP4, or OP5 closure promotion without explicit closure grammar and zero-register conditions.
- No physical overclaim from a structural/readout-local result.
- No global theorem from a local grammar result.
- No deletion of historical hypotheses without a traceable replacement.
