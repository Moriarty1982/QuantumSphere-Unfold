## How to read each statement

The Companion separates **conjecture** (the starting hypothesis), **mathematics** (a definition, theorem or construction built in the text) and **status** (proved, local or conditional, numerically checked, refuted, or open). Building mathematics from a conjecture does not prove the conjecture. Whether a unified theory follows is open.

## The starting conjecture

Spacetime and particles are projections of an underlying information layer. Matter, light (the photon) and a tachyon sector are meant to be *one* object seen from three frames. Spacetime points were to come from a "clock", originally the zeros of the Riemann zeta function. **Status:** conjecture. Since v4.8.0-r04 the Riemann paper is no longer a proof source; its proof chain for the Riemann Hypothesis does not hold.

## 1. The language: QAM over ZF

States, layers, operators, readouts and branches are written in a coded language, the **QAM**, defined on top of Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory by eight tagged tuple predicates. A finite instruction language (MML) and its execution semantics (MMA) sit above it. **Status:** clean definitions, recognition clauses rather than a new axiom system; translation back to set theory is shown for the admissible fragment.

**Executable example, 21 November 2026.** QAM holds the program and initial state of the binary tape model from 19K–19L. After guard checks, the MML instruction `RUN_TAPE` runs the machine within declared bounds on a fixed or right-extensible tape. MMA stores a replayable receipt. Wolfram Language independently checks tape growth, and Qiskit checks the reversible step on a fixed tape. The identification of the spinorial support and HC return remains conditional. [Code and instructions](https://github.com/Moriarty1982/QuantumSphere-Unfold/tree/main/qam/examples/zuse_machine)

**Hardware comparison.** Two SystemVerilog controllers produced the same traces and stop reasons as MMA in 209 bounded tests. Incrementing seven by one took four clock cycles with direct rule lookup and twenty-four with a cyclic scan of the six table slots. The measurement begins after loading the initial configuration. [Simulation and run report](https://github.com/Moriarty1982/QuantumSphere-Unfold/tree/main/qam/examples/zuse_machine/hdl)

**Quantum coprocessor (plan).** A classical accelerator is planned to recompute quantum circuits exactly. It takes a tape of instructions as input, runs on it autonomously and enforces the MMA guards in hardware: on a GPU for size, and in HDL/FPGA for an exact, checkable core. The first stage computes two qubits exactly in $\mathbb Z[e^{i\pi/4}]/\sqrt2^{k}$ and matched an exact reference and Qiskit in 2005 cases, and Wolfram exactly in six. Since 27 Nov 2026 the core synthesizes (Yosys check of all HDL blocks in CI) and has configuration registers: runtime limits (allowed instructions, guard width, k, gate budget) and a bank for still-uncertain quantities (β_sym, ε, axis tilt χ, δ_ν, band weight, offset x, strengths for rotation, band change and noise), set from outside and ready for the later compute units. A bridge translates OpenQASM (i.e. Qiskit circuits) to the instruction tape and back and exports states exactly for Wolfram and as Qiskit statevectors; 300 circuits match Qiskit, eight match Wolfram exactly. A general n-qubit state remains exponentially large; the tower and the Dixon algebra compress only structured states (products, symmetric subspaces). ## 2. The building block: three complex kernels

Each level has a biquaternion kernel $q = it + z_1e_1 + z_2e_2 + z_3e_3$, $e_3 = e_1e_2$. An involution $J$ splits every state into a symmetric part $C(f) = \tfrac12(f+Jf)$ (the **Heisenberg compensator**, $C^2 = C$) and an antisymmetric part. Since v4.8.0-r07 the sector is given by the sign of $s = \sum z_k^2$: matter $s > 0$, tachyon $s < 0$, light $s = 0$; the original norm had the wrong signature (1,6) and was replaced ("reading B"). **Status:** decomposition and sector rule are sound; that the sectors *are* matter, tachyon and light is conjecture.

## 3. Hopf structure and the frame constants

The three levels are linked to the classical Hopf fibrations over $S^2$, $S^4$, $S^8$. The central number is the **frame offset** $\beta_{\mathrm{sym}} = \sqrt{(\gamma_L-1)/(\gamma_L+1)} \approx 0.5494$ with $\gamma_L = \gamma_{\mathrm{BI}}/\gamma_{\mathrm{string}} \approx 1.8644$, the ratio of two published Barbero–Immirzi values (0.2375 and $\ln2/(\pi\sqrt3) \approx 0.1274$). **Status:** the fibrations are classical; their particle assignment is conjecture. $\beta_{\mathrm{sym}}$ is inserted, not derived. Since r63 it is read as a ratio of two horizon state countings, which works only with the U(1) counting; the literature prefers SU(2), which gives 0.604 (r68). The old label of the fibre $S^7$ as a group containing $G_2 \supset SU(3)$ was wrong and has been corrected.

## 4. Trioles and 5. Fractal light axis

The basic structure is threefold: three equal complex numbers 120° apart sum to zero. The triolic orbit scan is a well-defined local readout model, proved within its frame. "Triole" names three different structures (the frames M/P/T, the 120° kernels, and the three places of the octonionic triality form). The null axis ($s = 0$) is claimed to be transported from level to level and to be the light axis; that transport is explicitly open, and the diagnostic dimension $D_{\mathrm{res}} \approx 2.435$ is not a proof.

## 6. The master equation

$\mathfrak G + \mathfrak T_{\mathrm{tri}} + \mathfrak H_{\mathrm{HC}} + \mathfrak Q_{\mathrm{cl}} + \mathfrak B_\beta = 0$ organizes geometry, triole dynamics, compensator, closure and frame phase, with the 16-dimensional compensator matrix (called the Millennium matrix until r75) in the chain. **Status:** an outline of what should work together; couplings undetermined, no defined action. Research sections test what follows: commuting operators do not give a single eigenspace; direct compensator-to-mass routes are refuted one by one; a cyclic coupling gives the Brannen form but leaves masses underdetermined; scales stay free inputs; a geometry rebuilt from spinors is conditional and not a field solution.

## 7. Open problems OP1–OP5

OP1: derive $\beta_{\mathrm{sym}}$ (open; now "justify the two counting rules"). OP2: Dirac operator on the antisymmetric sector (old proofs rest on the Riemann paper). OP3: admissible shell passages (local QAM certificate). OP4: origin of the mixing angles (open; mis-posed in its old form, r61). OP5: cosmological constant (closed only locally by a detector threshold; exact form open). ## 8. Physical readouts

The old chain sets $\Lambda$ to zero in the photon frame and explains the observed value as a matter-frame effect. Several links fail: the trace formula is not a defined action (v4.8.0-r05), $\beta^2/\ell_P^2$ is $10^{121}$ too large, and one corollary gives zero and a finite ratio $\gamma_L^5$ for the same quantity. What survives is the share reading $\Omega_M = \beta_{\mathrm{sym}}^2 \approx 0.302$ (Planck 2018: 0.315 ± 0.007; DESI DR2: 0.2975 ± 0.0086), valid for today only (r67). The printed regularization was wrong; the corrected finite part at $T = 0.393$, $\mu = 1$ is 1.686953 (r69, corrected in r70). None of the old predictions P1–P6 is confirmed.

For the bent octant, magnitudes and endpoints fix the size $\pi/4$ of the step but not its sign, and the direct compensator has rank zero on the closed odd states (H113–H117, not blind); the selection of the ordered return remains open. The physics paper gains two development subsections on these points.

Layer v4.10.0-r31 records the work after v5.3.0. The octant phase is τ·ε·π/4; nothing in the Companion fixes its sign, and time reversal of the field amplitudes (complex conjugation on χ) reverses it while parity keeps it, so the sign is a time orientation of the readout (H118); OP3 rests by the author's decision. In W1 with a Holst term, real and complex boosts leave the Immirzi parameter unchanged; only a duality rotation of the curvature changes it, arctan γ ↦ arctan γ + θ, and since every such rotation preserves A² + B², a frame-dependent γ at equal Newton constant (γ_L) is excluded.

Layer v4.10.0-r32 is the source for the worked OP5 case: an elastically prepared compression of matter produces a Higgs source whose ordinary linear gravitational response vanishes outside the support (retarded solution), while a directed contrast 16𝒜P/(m²R⁴) remains on the same measurement axis if the Higgs source is identified with the OP5 drive; that identification is an assumption without an action. The physics paper (section 6.9) and the German preprint (appendix B) cite this layer.

Layer v4.10.0-r33 tests the source identification of the OP5 case against the Higgs sector of W2: it holds exactly for the scalar channel if the comparison field is the Higgs radial mode, but that reading forces a Lorentz-invariant scalar and, for minimal coupling, excludes the directed mediator on which the contrast depends. The open assumption is now the existence of a subluminal massive mediator coupled linearly to the Higgs radius.

Layer v4.10.0-r34 searches the Companion for that mediator. No vacuum field meets all requirements. The counter-propagating pair connector of a matter state and its tachyon image is massive, subluminal (|v| = e^{−2η}) and directed, but has no action and no established coupling to the Higgs radius. The optical mode of the two-component material meets every requirement, couples only if Y₁/ρ₁ ≠ Y₂/ρ₂, and would make the directed contrast a property of the preparation.

Layer v4.10.0-r35 works out that material route. A directed mediator needs a distinguished frame, which here comes from the sample. A uniaxial two-component preparation gives a directed massive term (mass = optical gap) that couples only if the components carry different Higgs fractions of their rest mass. The leading contrast does not need the subluminal coefficient; the numerator is one power of the transverse wave number short of the comparison model. The route carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r36 tries a vacuum background instead. Without a background no linear coupling of a new field to |Φ|² gives a directed pole (the vector and tensor exchanges reduce to contact terms). With a frame and an axis, a spin-2 field with a positive polarization sum reproduces the numerator of the comparison model exactly. Frame, axis and polarization structure are chosen, not derived; the route carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r37 follows the deferred idea I1: a string stretched between the matter and the tachyon system, with a tension that depends on the Higgs radius. The string supplies frame, axis and polarization structure itself. Its two-quantum states reproduce both directed parts of the numerator in the exact ratio; the undirected part is half as large, and a second pole appears at half the mass. The route carries partially; the tension law is assumed.

Layer v4.10.0-r38 gives the mediator a first set of field equations: a tensor field transverse to the axis and a scalar, both coupled to the Higgs radial mode. Their exchange reproduces the mediator term of the comparison model exactly, with unique couplings, and the trace coupling it needs (κ = ½) is the value the string gives. The ansatz is free of ghosts and statically stable exactly if g_m² ≤ 3r_m. The scalar is still set; the ansatz carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r39 obtains the scalar as a component of one tensor field on the subspace orthogonal to the axis. With one mass and the identically conserved coupling ∂_a∂_bη − η_ab□_∥η (the version on that subspace of the improvement behind the comparison operator) the exchange is exact again, provided the time–transverse components do not couple, which is a choice. The decomposition differs from that of the string; the result carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r40 tests reasons for that decoupling. No dynamics tested produces it: a Fierz–Pauli field on the subspace does not see the conserved source, and auxiliary time–transverse components add a second pole. It follows exactly if the exchange weights both relative time orientations of its ends equally, because the time–transverse vertices are odd under time reversal. This is the same time reversal that reverses the orientation of the octant (H118). The equal weighting is not derived; the result carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r41 finds the weighting nearly realized in the OP5 seed: its counterflow block is, to 8·10⁻⁴, the local time-reversed image of the co-moving blocks, and the densities of the two directions agree to better than 10⁻³. If the ends of the exchange are read out in these currents, the decoupling holds almost exactly. The seed does not distinguish time reversal from reflection; time reversal is chosen because only it gives the mediator term. The result carries partially.

Layer v4.10.0-r42 tests the first derivatives at the seed. There the counterflow block is neither the time-reversed nor the reflected image of the co-moving blocks, nor a plain reversal of the currents. The relation of r41 holds for the values at the seed point only; the jet does not decide the question. The jet does not satisfy all conditions of the model.

Layer v4.10.0-r43 shows that the model conditions fix only 31 of the 96 first derivatives. The remaining family contains a jet with an exact time-reversed image and one with an exact reflected image. The first jet therefore does not decide; the finding of r42 concerned the stored solution only.

Layer v4.10.0-r44 shows that both image jets extend to a second jet, as do random members of the family. The second order does not separate time reversal from reflection either; the levels available at the seed are exhausted.

Layer v4.10.0-r45 audits this chain. The directed term was originally an ordinary Fierz–Pauli exchange of a set, directed source; there the time–transverse components couple and are needed. The decoupling question of r39–r44 arises in one decomposition only. What remains set is the frame, the axis and the slowed propagation of the coupling. The recurring candidates are four ways of supplying this frame; behind them lies a fork between the field side and the readout side, which is the author's decision.

Layer v4.10.0-r46 follows the readout side. With the frame and axis read as the rest frame and measuring axis of a detector, the directed coupling is exactly that detector's readout of the tidal field transverse to its axis. The time orientation drops out, the slowed propagation is not needed for the leading contrast, and a prediction follows: the contrast depends weakly on the motion of the detector. A massive spin-2 mediator and its coupling remain set.

Layer v4.10.0-r47 corrects r46: the slowed propagation is needed after all, not for the contrast but for causality. With luminal mediators, already with an ordinary graviton, disturbances of the coupled system travel faster than light. The slowing remains set and needs a distinguished frame on the field side, so the readout branch carries only partially. A bigravity of M and T fails the same condition.

Layer v4.10.0-r48 compares this readout with the observer reading of OP1. Both take the electric part of a field strength in the observer's rest frame, and in both the time orientation drops out. In OP1, however, the rest frame and the axis belong to the field; in the comparison model the axis belongs to the detector. The field rest frame of OP1 is thereby a named, untested candidate for the frame the slowed propagation needs.

Layer v4.10.0-r49 tests it. The field is unchanged by boosts along its axis, so it can slow propagation only across the axis; along the axis the cone limit fails. Found afterwards: with the luminal Higgs radius of W2 the directed coupling is causally incompatible with every choice of mediator. Three propagations would have to be slowed, which needs a medium with its own rest frame.

Layer v4.10.0-r50 (H120) tests a medium built from light-like streams of the photon frame. One stream leaves its direction luminal; two counter-streams form a medium only near the frame in which they balance and degenerate into one stream towards the photon frame. For any medium the required slowing is at least √(7g_m²/6); since laboratory bounds exclude Lorentz violation above about 10⁻¹³, the directed coupling is practically excluded in vacuum, and an observable contrast would need a material.

Layer v4.10.0-r51 tests the bending by the frame map J_B between M and T. J_B fixes the light-like directions along the M–T axis but gives a medium built from them the opposite sign: what narrows the light cone in M widens it beyond the speed of light in T. No medium is causal in both frames at once; this reading does not carry.
