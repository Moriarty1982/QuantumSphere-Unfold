\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Starting from the hypothesis that matter, light and a tachyon sector are one object in three frames, we have shown that a complex
quaternion kernel carries this hypothesis in a precise form.  Its natural norm is the Lorentz invariant of an electromagnetic field; the
duality rotation exchanges matter-type and tachyon-type configurations and fixes the light-like ones; the symmetric triple of kernels is the
centred null configuration; and a compensator built from the duality involution splits each field into two ports, which on the real doublet
places matter and tachyon on opposite sides of a state disk with light at its centre.

On \(\mathbb C\otimes\HH\otimes\OO\) a projector of the same form yields the chirality of one Standard Model generation with right-handed
neutrino, and within the stated setting anomaly cancellation alone selects it, up to the frame change.  The single numerical input, the
frame offset \(\beta_{\rm sym}\approx0.549\), can be read as a ratio of two horizon countings selected by uncertainty saturation; it gives four
predictions that were registered in advance and are pending.

The framework does not yet contain a dynamics.  We have stated what one would have to do, and which of the present results would change
status if it were found.  We hope that the structural results, in particular the chirality selection, are useful independently of the
hypothesis that motivated them.
