\section{Method}\label{app:method}

\paragraph{Pre-registration.}  Since October 2026 every test with a decision criterion has been written down before the computation
as a note giving the hypothesis, the data, and the criterion, and committed to the public repository~\cite{repo}; the commit time stamps
it.  Fifty-eight such tests have been registered so far.  Notes that were not blind --- because the target window or a nearby value
was known --- say so, and such results are not counted as confirmations.  Explorations without a note are labelled as explorations.
Tests whose criterion could not be met by the method are recorded as invalid, not as passed.

\paragraph{Append-only record.}  Results are added as numbered rounds to the longer version~\cite{companion}; earlier rounds are never
rewritten, and a correction is a new round that names what it corrects.

\paragraph{Scope list.}  A fixed list of statements that are not claimed --- among them any exact global closure and any statement about the
Riemann hypothesis --- is checked against every round, also mechanically by a linter over the source.

\paragraph{Formalization.}  Parts of the algebra are formalized in Lean~4 with Mathlib and built in continuous integration without
\texttt{sorry}: among them the compensator and the doublet matrices (Proposition~\ref{prop:doublet}), the Gram decomposition of the state
disk, the cyclic invariance of the triality form, the sector involutions on \(\mathbb C\otimes\OO\), and the uncertainty saturation of
Proposition~\ref{prop:robertson}.

\paragraph{Recomputation.}  Results that carry weight are recomputed with independently written code, at higher precision where
needed, and in one series cross-checked between different language models.  Two readings were corrected this
way (Appendix~\ref{app:earlier}); in another case a reported high-precision finding was traced to a conversion error and withdrawn before it
entered a round.  Large rank computations can be distributed over parallel runners, one order per job.

\paragraph{Use of language models.}  The starting conjecture --- that matter, light and a tachyon sector are one object in three
frames --- is the author's.  Everything else grew out of it in a continuous dialogue with large language models (Claude, Anthropic;
ChatGPT and Codex, OpenAI), used from the beginning.  Some of the mathematical tools were proposed by the models, others came from ideas
the author contributed; the author chose the questions, decided which readings to test and which results to keep.  The models wrote and
ran the numerical code, produced the Lean formalization, searched the literature and drafted much of the text, including this paper.
Their output was accepted only through the checks described above, different models were used to cross-check each other, and several of
their results were withdrawn by these checks.  The author takes full responsibility for the content.
