\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have studied a complex quaternion kernel whose natural norm is the Lorentz invariant of an electromagnetic field.  Its positive and
negative sectors form one orbit of the Lorentz and duality group, the null sector is the only non-trivial orbit fixed by duality, the
symmetric triple of components is the centred null configuration, and the involution that defines the compensator exchanges the two sectors
and contains time reversal.  On \(\mathbb C\otimes\HH\otimes\OO\) a projector of the same form restricts the weak \(SU(2)\) to the Pati--Salam
\(\mathbf4\) and completes one Standard Model generation with right-handed neutrino; among the four projectors compatible with colour and charge,
anomaly cancellation selects it up to time reversal.

These results give a precise form to the hypothesis that matter, light and a tachyon sector are three readouts of one object, differing in
the direction of time: the structure shows the sectors alike and related by maps that contain time reversal.  They do not yet go beyond the
Feynman--St\"uckelberg reading of antiparticles.  What would make the hypothesis physical --- a dynamics in which the time-reversed reading
propagates space-like --- is open, and so is the origin of the single number on which the speculative readouts of Section~\ref{sec:pheno} depend.
