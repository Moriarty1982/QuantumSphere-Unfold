# Relative probe motion: longitudinal restriction and transverse freedom

2026-10-27. Exact local bilinear analysis, not solved probe evolution or geometry.

Take A and B with parallel rest spin in the z direction and combined rest density n_p. The third probe C has opposite rest spin and density n_c. Use pure positive-energy rest spinors boosted locally; vector and axial bilinears transform as Lorentz vectors. A and B share one boost in this comparison, C may have another. No identification of these boosts with the native matter/tachyon frames is made.

## Longitudinal motion does not supply the hoped-for freedom

For boosts along z with rapidities r_p and r_c, the total axial time/z components are proportional to

 (n_p sinh r_p-n_c sinh r_c,
  n_p cosh r_p-n_c cosh r_c).

Zero total axial current with positive densities forces tanh r_p=tanh r_c, hence r_p=r_c and n_p=n_c. Merely assigning different longitudinal speeds to the compensating third mode therefore breaks exact spin cancellation in this ansatz.

## Transverse relative motion is genuinely available

For boosts along x, an axial rest vector along z is unchanged by the boost. Thus n_p=n_c=n cancels total axial spin for ANY two transverse rapidities r_p,r_c. The total vector current is

 J=n(cosh r_p+cosh r_c, sinh r_p+sinh r_c,0,0).

For equal A/B amplitudes and phase, the selected cross current is

 j_S=(n/2)(cosh r_p,sinh r_p,0,0).

For counterflow r_c=-r_p=r, total spatial current is zero while selected cross spatial current is nonzero:

 J=(2n cosh r,0,0,0),
 j_S=(n cosh r/2,n sinh r/2,0,0).

At fixed total density N=2n cosh r, this is j_S=(N/4,(N/4)tanh r,0,0). Thus the third mode can balance total momentum/current and spin without eliminating the relative spatial cross channel. This breaks the previous common-boost relation j_S=J/4. Each constituent current remains future causal for finite rapidity; this is not a faster signal claim.

## Important control

At fixed N the counterflow example has constant cross time component N/4. It therefore does NOT reproduce j_S0=2t with a fixed time coframe. A space/time-dependent rapidity creates spatial cross-current gradients that may contribute to a DIFFERENT coframe; rank, full Ward identity, connection compatibility and probe stress must be recomputed. Pointwise cancellation of total spatial current is not a proof of the field equations. Using the old j_S, connection or metric unchanged would discard precisely the new relative-current contribution.

Result: the first nontrivial relative-motion route consistent with the simple spin balance is transverse, not arbitrary independent longitudinal motion. This is a concrete admissible bilinear direction for the native third mode, still awaiting a derived dynamical coupling and common geometry. OP5 remains open.
