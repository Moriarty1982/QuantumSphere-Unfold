## Precise hypotheses

 E^a=(1/2) D_omega j_S^a,

For Lorentz connection omega, curvature R^a_b=domega^a_b+omega^a_c wedge omega^c_b and torsion T^a=D_omega E^a, direct differentiation gives

Thus arbitrary spin curvature cannot be inserted into the readout while retaining this exact dictionary and torsion-free compatibility. Nonzero torsion is an alternate model possibility, not automatically ordinary torsion-free GR and not determined without its action.

## Implication for an ordinary Einstein vacuum region

With the tetrad postulate and torsion-free metric connection, E=(1/2)D j implies nabla_mu j^nu=2 delta_mu^nu and R^rho_(sigma mu nu)j^sigma=0, hence Ric_mu_nu j^nu=0. Assume an ordinary independent-metric Einstein vacuum equation Ric_mu_nu=Lambda_geom g_mu_nu in four dimensions. Then Lambda_geom j_mu=0. On any region where j is nonzero this forces Lambda_geom=0.

This is a compatibility/exclusion statement: the dictionary plus torsion-free ordinary vacuum equation excludes a nonzero cosmological constant on that region. It does NOT show dynamical cancellation of arbitrary vacuum energy. A composite/multiplier action can change the effective metric equation through reaction terms, so its equations cannot be silently replaced by the independent Einstein vacuum equation.
