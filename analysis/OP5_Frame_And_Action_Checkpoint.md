## Evidence and limitations

Exact analytic derivations and counterexamples are distinguished from finite symbolic/numerical controls in the individual notes. The included *_Check.py scripts reproduce the saved component, evolution, clock and connection checks. Some short symbolic controls were run interactively and are documented by their formulas rather than separate scripts. No independent review of this checkpoint is claimed. No native common action, detector identification, quantum completion, universal speed derivation or physical vacuum-energy selection has been established. Earlier local detector-level closure is not revoked or promoted into global physical closure.

The latest question is whether the connection equation of a specified common action permits torsion-free compatibility or carries spin/multiplier sources. The original paper's assumptions cannot be silently substituted for that variation.
