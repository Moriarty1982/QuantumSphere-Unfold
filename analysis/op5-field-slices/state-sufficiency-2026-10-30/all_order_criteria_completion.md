# The fifteenth completed-system identity and conditional first projection

30 October 2026. Bounded continuation of route C. This document derives the missing scalar relation for the first prolongation of R=(F24,G4,C4), C_mu=partial_mu g. It does not prove all nonlinear prolongation projections or OP5. Parent numerical records completed_tableau_check.py/result and completed-symbol-tower-result.json are evidence candidates, not exact rank or root certificates supplied by this note.

## 1. Setup and exact identities already established

Fix a regular first jet, E invertible, A rank4, and the symbol nondegeneracy condition in all_order_criteria.md. All derivatives below are ordinary coordinate derivatives; all jets are symmetric actual derivatives of a local Taylor field.

Let V=E^-1 and let D^mu be the principal Clifford factor, so J^mu=D^mu P. Set K_a^mu=(A Dbar^mu)_a. The flat-coordinate identity eta^T K=q ell becomes

sym_(mu,nu) sum_a V^(mu a) K_a^nu = q^(mu nu) ell,

where q^(mu nu)=sum_a signature_a V^(mu a)V^(nu a), signature=(+,-,-,-). This q is minus the paper's (-,+,+,+) inverse metric convention. Consequently

g=ell F,

C_nu=partial_nu g=(partial_nu ell)F+ell partial_nu F,

G_a=sum_nu K_a^nu partial_nu F.

The last expression contains no second jets because the symmetric highest terms cancel. Thus G and C are first-order functions. The completed first-jet manifold is R1={F=0,G=0,C=0}; F=0 implies g=0.

## 2. Exact operator lift of the quadratic symbol relation

Differentiating the preceding identities and cancelling symmetric second derivatives of F gives, for every local smooth field,

sum_(mu,a) V^(mu a) partial_mu G_a - sum_(mu,nu) q^(mu nu) partial_mu C_nu

= sum_nu M^nu partial_nu F - sum_(mu,nu) q^(mu nu)(partial_mu partial_nu ell)F,

where

M^nu = sum_(mu,a) V^(mu a)partial_mu K_a^nu -2 sum_mu q^(mu nu)partial_mu ell.

No derivatives of V or q appear on the left because the operator is written V partial G -q partial C, not in divergence form. Rewriting it in divergence form would add the corresponding coefficient derivatives.

Important: M can depend on the second jet, since K depends on V and V depends on first jets. The displayed identity alone must therefore not be advertised as a first-order differential identity with coefficients depending only on the first jet. The reduction in the next section handles this issue.

## 3. Restriction to R1 and an explicit missing left row

Fix a base first jet in R1. Let z denote a symmetric second jet (240 real entries). Write

u(z)=dF(z)=A_F z+b_F,

Y(z)=(dG(z),dC(z))=B z+b_Y.

Both maps are affine in z because F,G,C are first-order functions. The original symbol A_F has rank88 and its complete eight-dimensional left space is generated by four ell dF rows and four K dF rows. On R1 their sources vanish by C=G=F=0. Thus an affine reference second jet z0 with u(z0)=0 exists under the original symbol hypotheses. No arbitrary regional solution is being assumed: this is only the solvable original first-prolongation linear system.

Define the fixed row r on the32 entries of Y by

rY = sum_(mu,a) V^(mu a)partial_mu G_a -sum_(mu,nu) q^(mu nu)partial_mu C_nu.

The operator lift on F=0 says rY(z)=M(z)u(z). At z0 the right side vanishes. Differentiate this algebraic identity with respect to z at z0. Since u(z0)=0, the derivative-of-M term disappears, giving

r B = M(z0) A_F.

As the left side and u(z) are affine, equality of their derivatives plus equality at z0 gives the identity for every symmetric z:

rY(z)-M(z0)u(z)=0.

Therefore the missing fifteenth left row is explicitly

(-M(z0), V,-q)

on the concatenated blocks (dF,dG,dC). Its coefficient vector depends only on the chosen reference z0 and the fixed base first jet, not on the variable z. In a constant-rank chart z0 can be selected analytically from the original affine solve. Different choices of z0 produce rows differing by original left relations; the new class modulo the original relations is well-defined.

This identity is asserted on R1. Off shell the term -q(partial²ell)F and the derivative-of-M contribution at a reference with u!=0 cannot be discarded. Numeric use near R1 must retain or bound these lower-residual terms.

## 4. Why there are exactly fifteen compatibility rows under the observed rank hypothesis

The first completed prolongation has128 equations and240 second-jet variables. There are:

1. eight compatibility rows of the dF block;
2. six exact skew rows partial_mu C_nu-partial_nu C_mu=0 because C=dg;
3. the row from section3.

These15 rows are independent: the first eight live in dF, the six skew rows add independent dC entries, and the last row has a nonzero dG block V, independent of the others. The latter nonzero block follows from invertible V. If the completed second-jet matrix has rank113, these rows exhaust its left kernel. Numerical parent calculations suggest precisely rank113, but a nonzero exact/validated113-minor or an analytic rank argument is still needed to certify application at an exact root.

## 5. Conditional projection-surjectivity proposition

**Proposition.** Suppose a regular first jet belongs exactly to R1, the original symbol hypotheses hold (so rank A_F=88 and its eight compatibility rows are complete), and the completed second-jet matrix has rank113. Then there is a symmetric second jet satisfying dF=dG=dC=0. If R1 is a smooth analytic manifold and these ranks remain constant on a neighborhood, the completed second-order equation is locally an analytic affine bundle over R1 with fibre dimension127; its projection is a submersion.

**Proof.** Choose z0 with u(z0)=0 as above. The16 entries of dC obey six skew relations; the operator lift additionally gives rY(z0)=0. These seven independent relations define a25-dimensional linear subspace Z of the32-component Y space. For every delta z in ker A_F, both Y(z0+delta z) and its variation belong to Z.

The rank of the restricted map B|ker A_F equals rank([A_F;B])-rank A_F=113-88=25. Hence its image is all of Z. Since Y(z0) belongs to Z, choose delta z in ker A_F with B delta z=-Y(z0). Then z0+delta z solves every completed derivative equation. Constant-rank analytic linear algebra supplies local analytic sections and the claimed127-dimensional affine fibres. End proof.

This proof uses the on-shell operator identity to remove the apparent extra scalar obstruction. It does not infer surjectivity merely from one numerical tangent-gate norm or from matching dimensions. The source membership in Z is the decisive additional input.

## 6. Smoothness of R1: a useful conditional reduction

The completed R has32 scalar components. Its first-jet-only Jacobian is numerically reported to have rank31. The original identity ell F=g gives a left row of this first-jet Jacobian and, on F=0, its derivative in field-value directions is the nonzero gradient of g. On g=0 with h!=0, q_scalar=Psi†ZbetaPsi=8/h!=0, so that gradient cannot vanish. Thus rank31 of the first-jet part implies full rank32 with field values included. If those ranks are established at an exact root, the implicit-function theorem gives a smooth analytic R1 chart. Approximate residuals alone do not establish existence of that exact root.
