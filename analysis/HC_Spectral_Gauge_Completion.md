## 2. Fixed Observable Versus Covariantly Transformed Readout

Suppose the bare expression `Re(x^dagger R_12 y)` must be invariant for arbitrary allowed probe values and first jets, with R_12 fixed. At a point the covariant derivative jet y transforms like the probe x. For an internal unitary g, invariance therefore requires

\[
 \operatorname{Re}\{x^\dagger(g^\dagger R_{12}g-R_{12})y\}=0
 \quad\text{for all }x,y.
\]

**Proposition.** Under that state-independent fixed-observable contract, `g^dagger R_12 g=R_12`. Infinitesimally every allowed Hermitian generator commutes with R_12.

**Proof.** Set `A=g^dagger R_12 g-R_12`. For any y, choose x=Ay. The real pairing is `||Ay||^2`, so it can vanish for all jets only if A=0. In the spinorial expression an invertible Clifford weight and independently variable spin jets supply the same internal test. Restricting to the value of one on-shell probe would be a weaker condition and would not justify this conclusion.

For the prepared fixed resonance `Omega=I2 tensor M_triangle`, invariance of both Omega and R_12 gives the **common** algebra without postulating separate channel-label projectors:

\[
 \{T=T^\dagger:[T,\Omega]=[T,R_{12}]=0\}
 =\{Z\otimes I_3:Z=Z^\dagger\}.                          \tag{1}
\]

**Finite proof.** Hermitian T commuting with R_12 also commutes with its adjoint. Their products yield the channel projectors R_11 and R_22; subtraction from I gives R_33. For the nonzero scalar links 4 and .7, `R_11 Omega R_33/4=I2 tensor |1><3|` and `R_22 Omega R_33/.7=I2 tensor |2><3|`. Products and adjoints generate all channel matrix units. Their joint commutant is `M2 tensor I3`; taking Hermitian elements gives (1), of real dimension four. Thus fixed-label conditions follow from this **stronger observable contract**, not from resonance or C7 closure alone. The values 4 and .7 are still comparison inputs, not native operator elements.

If instead the readout reference is transformed as `R_12->g R_12 g^dagger`, the pairing is invariant for any g with the jointly transformed data. Then (1) need not hold. The reference may be part of a covariant readout dictionary; that is not automatically an additional dynamical field or a source current. Its admissibility and interpretation must be specified.

A direct witness uses a phase transformation generated by one resonance spectral projector. It commutes with Omega but not R_12. For the generic fixed probe-jet test in the code the real cross pairing changes from `1` to `.9616684817`; transforming the readout too restores `1`.
