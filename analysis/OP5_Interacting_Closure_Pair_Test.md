# Beyond one-probe closure: an exact pair-transfer counterexample

2026-10-27. Scope audit of OP5_Protected_Clock_And_Detector.md. Auxiliary quantum-mode calculation; no claim of native hidden particles or actual faster signaling.

## The missing many-excitation condition

The preceding protected-clock witness proves closure for its declared block-diagonal one-probe Hamiltonian and detector interaction. It remains correct under those assumptions. It does not establish closure after arbitrary interactions or geometric backreaction. A one-particle symmetry J does not automatically protect the many-particle subspace with no hidden excitations.

Let a,b be bosonic visible/hidden mode operators and N_b=b^dagger b. Define hidden parity J_F=exp(i pi N_b). Consider the Hermitian interaction

 H_int=lambda(a^dagger^2 b^2+b^dagger^2 a^2), lambda real.

It conserves total occupation N_a+N_b and commutes with J_F because hidden occupation changes only by two. Thus it respects the sign-flip symmetry of the hidden field. Nevertheless

 H_int |2,0>=2lambda |0,2>,
 H_int |0,2>=2lambda |2,0>.

On this two-dimensional sector, with equal unperturbed pair energies (resonance), the exact evolution from |2,0> has hidden-pair probability sin^2(2lambda t). The initial and final states both have EVEN hidden parity, but only the initial state has hidden vacuum. The full even-parity sector is invariant; the smaller hidden-vacuum sector is not. Equal free frequencies supply resonance in this counterexample; detuning changes the transfer amplitude and does not restore exact isolation for nonzero coupling.

In particular P_even=(I+J_F)/2 is NOT Pi_visible=I_visible tensor |0_hidden><0_hidden|. Reusing the same symbol C or P for these different spaces would hide the gap. The exact interacting protection condition is (I-Pi_visible) H_total Pi_visible=0, including source, detector and geometric interactions on the full declared state space.

The analogous local field interaction phi^2 chi^2 is even in hidden chi. A classical chi=0 configuration can remain a solution because its field equation has no constant source at chi=0, yet that fact alone does not exclude quantum pair creation or the growth of hidden perturbations. Stability, occupation production and causal support are separate tests. Bosonic modes are a minimal counterexample to the unrestricted symmetry inference; no identification with native fermionic probe modes is made.

## Relation to geometry and OP5

A common dynamical geometry can introduce allowed interactions between sectors; whether it does so in the native model must be derived from its action. We have not calculated a native pair-production amplitude. The counterexample only shows that J-invariance or classical sector preservation is insufficient as a general guarantee of exact operational isolation once interactions are admitted.

There is a further independent distinction: absence of real hidden excitations would not by itself remove vacuum contributions of a field coupled to geometry. No inference from protected signals to zero gravitational vacuum term is valid without the effective action and its renormalization/variation. This note does not calculate such contributions.

The next native target is therefore the full interaction action and its state-space meaning: classical probe theory versus quantized fields, admissible collective couplings and gravitational coupling. A complete closure claim must specify which of these levels it covers. This narrows the assumption set; it does not refute the earlier finite-dimensional clock witness or establish a hidden sector in nature.
