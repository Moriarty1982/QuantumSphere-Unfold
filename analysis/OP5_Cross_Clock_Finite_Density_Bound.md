# Clock reparametrization and a finite-density bound

2026-10-27. Continuation of OP5_Triadic_Conserved_Density_Test.md; all conclusions conditional on the homogeneous rest-current dictionary, not a general cosmological theorem.

## Relabelling time does not supply a new exchange law

Take the conserved-density spin-balanced triad with constant coordinate-time rotation theta=g t and cross current J=N sin(2gt)/4. With vanishing spatial cross current and Lorentz compatibility, the time component of E=(1/2)D j gives E0=(J'/2)dt. Thus the induced proper time on a positive-lapse patch is

 tau=J/2=N sin(2gt)/8,
 d tau/dt=N g cos(2gt)/4.

For a constant spatial coframe one can choose omega^i_0=2 dx^i/J on J!=0, obtaining the earlier geometric torsion witness in tau coordinates. But the exchange rate in proper time is

 d theta/d tau=4/[N cos(2gt)]
             =4/[N sqrt(1-(8tau/N)^2)]

on the branch cos(2gt)>0. This is precisely the previously inverse-engineered nonconstant coupling. Calling g constant in coordinate time does not turn it into a constant physical proper-time coupling. The positive-lapse branch reaches tau=N/8 where the lapse vanishes; repeated sine branches cannot be glued into a regular monotone clock without new structure. The J=0 endpoint also lies outside the chosen torsion-connection patch.

## Bound independent of the chosen rotation

For any two classical complex amplitudes in a positive local norm,

 |Re(A^dagger B)|<=||A|| ||B||<=(||A||^2+||B||^2)/2<=n_total/2.

In the restricted spin-balanced parallel-up/up/down triad, the third mode carries half the density, so the sharper bound is |J|<=n_total/4. These are algebraic bounds, independent of g or the chosen rotation profile. For the homogeneous rest cross current, E0=(1/2)dJ implies J=2tau+J0 on an oriented proper-time interval. Consequently finite constant n_total forbids an indefinitely growing proper-time interval with this unchanged dictionary. For J0=0 and the restricted triad, tau<=N/8. The general positive-norm bound gives tau<=N/4. An arbitrary shift J0 changes endpoints but does not remove bounded interval length for bounded density.

The positive classical-amplitude interpretation is essential. Renormalized quantum cross bilinears are not assumed to satisfy the same state-independent finite density bound. Nonzero spatial cross current or a changed readout can also change the time relation.

## Homogeneous expanding-volume control

With E0=d tau, Ei=a(tau)dxi, homogeneous total phase current at rest and no external charge exchange, the density-form Ward law implies a^3 n_total=N_comoving. Combining with the restricted triad bound and J=2tau gives

 a(tau)^3<=N_comoving/(8tau),  tau>0.

Thus an indefinitely expanding or positive-lower-bounded spatial volume cannot sustain this particular cross-clock assignment at conserved comoving charge. The inequality is an upper bound, not an equation forcing contraction at every instant. It is not evidence that the universe must contract: it rules out a restricted matter/clock/readout ansatz as a general expanding cosmology. Spatial transport, different current orientation, different source states, or a modified coframe dictionary require a fresh calculation.

## Result

The triadic spin balance removes the two-rest-probe algebraic spin obstruction, but finite conserved density supplies a further finite-range bound. Reparametrizing time moves the required variable exchange law into the lapse and does not eliminate it. The next source-backed route should allow spatially structured conserved probe flux and evaluate the full cross-vector, instead of fitting another homogeneous exchange profile. The field, connection and coframe equations remain unsolved together; no OP5 closure is asserted.
