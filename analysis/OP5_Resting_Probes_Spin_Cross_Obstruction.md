# Two resting positive-energy probes: spin cancellation versus cross clock

2026-10-27. Exact restricted obstruction for the torsionful witness. Not a no-go for general Dirac fields, mixed states or additional probe components.

## Why zero total axial spin is tested

The prior geometric witness has pure vector torsion. In the minimal independent-Lorentz-connection Dirac parent, the probe spin coefficient is totally antisymmetric. The vector Cartan source of that witness can be written as a multiplier wedge with the same nonzero j. Subtracting this particular multiplier from any other proposed multiplier leaves another wedge source. By the lemma in OP5_Dirac_Spin_Connection_Symmetry_Test.md, such a difference cannot be a nonzero totally antisymmetric tensor. Therefore matching that pure-vector source with only the minimal Dirac spin and this multiplier structure requires zero TOTAL Dirac spin coefficient. Other actions or tensor/axial torsion change this conclusion.

## Restricted rest ansatz

At a point in the standard Dirac representation take two commuting classical positive-beta spinors

 A=(a,0)^T, B=(b,0)^T, a,b in C^2.

Their total spatial axial spin is proportional to a^dagger sigma_i a+b^dagger sigma_i b. The time component of axial spin is zero in this ansatz. The cross vector has j_S^0=Re(a^dagger b) and j_S^i=0. We want a nonzero timelike cross current, in particular j_S^0=2t on t>0 in the torsion witness.

Let U=[a b] be the two by two matrix of the upper spinors. Zero total spatial spin implies

 U U^dagger=a a^dagger+b b^dagger=r I,
 r=(||a||^2+||b||^2)/2.

For r>0, U is invertible and U^dagger U=r I as well. Hence a^dagger b=0 and ||a||=||b||. For r=0 both vanish. Therefore zero total spin forces the cross time component to vanish. It cannot realize j_S^0=2t!=0 in this rest ansatz.

A simple control is opposite spin a=(1,0), b=(0,1): spin cancels but the cross clock vanishes. Parallel spin a=b=(1,0) gives a nonzero cross clock but nonzero total spin. Relative common phases do not change this obstruction; orthogonality remains orthogonality.

## Consequence

The pure-vector torsion geometry and its multiplier source are algebraically possible, but two pure resting positive-energy Dirac probes cannot supply its nonzero timelike cross current and required zero total spin simultaneously under the minimal source assumptions. This rejects a concrete simple dynamical realization, not the entire torsion route.

Remaining routes require a new evaluated ansatz: lower Dirac components/nontrivial momentum, a genuinely mixed/ensemble matter description with its appropriate bilinear interpretation, additional intrinsic triadic modes, nonminimal couplings, or torsion with further irreducible components. None is automatically supplied by renaming frames. The native third triadic probe is a source-supported candidate to examine before inventing new fields, but its inclusion changes which currents and spin sources are summed and must be explicit.

An elementary algebraic illustration with a THIRD positive-beta probe C=(c,0) is a=b=(1,0), c=(0,sqrt(2)). Then total spatial spin cancels while the selected A/B cross current remains nonzero. Scaling all three upper amplitudes by sqrt(2t) realizes selected j_S^0=2t and zero total spin at each point. This is a pointwise bilinear assignment only: derivatives, connection/probe equations, stress, native projector embedding and coframe reactions have not been solved. It shows why the native third component may be useful as a spin-balancing channel, rather than an inaccessible sector.
