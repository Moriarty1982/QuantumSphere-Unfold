Description:
Version 4.7.0 of the Quantum Sphaera Companion is a guarded, structure-first full-monolith release. It preserves the full proof/provenance spine while conservatively compressing release-safe audit and solve-pass layers. The release formalizes local/restricted/effective closure results, including conservative relative-seam treatment of the C4 obstruction, detector-threshold terminality for C5 at theta=1/256 after n=20, local photon-clock selector preservation under Delta_gamma<1/5, typed mixed-comparison local control, terminal-data and restricted naturality layers, matter-frame epsilon terminality, tachyon-frame signed-scale separation, and effective/local closure reclassification.

The r85 layer adds two source repairs found by a systematic cross-reference audit against the two cited source papers. Two passages that supported the Stone-von Neumann step with the wrong theorem number now cite the correct location, and the gauge group U(1)xSU(2)xSU(3) has been moved out of the unitary-equivalence list of the central unity theorem into a separate consequence, since a Lie group is not a Hilbert-space representation of the Weyl algebra and no Stone-von Neumann equivalence can be asserted for it. Both repairs narrow what is asserted; no result is withdrawn and the gauge-structure theorem is unchanged.

The release explicitly does not claim exact finite C5 zero closure, exact all-readout terminality, C6/T2 closure, a global photon-clock/RH theorem, or global all-readout closure. The previous/baseline DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20117361 is retained only as provenance.
