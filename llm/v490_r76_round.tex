\section*{v4.9.0-r76 Source-Only: Correction to r75 --- the Earlier Name ``Layer-Transport Matrix''}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{v4.9.0-r76 Source-Only: Correction to r75 --- the Earlier Name ``Layer-Transport Matrix''}
\label{sec:v490-r76-name-correction}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Purpose.}
Layer v4.9.0-r75 (\S\ref{sec:v490-r75-compensator-matrix}) renamed \(\mathcal M_{16}\) from ``Millennium matrix'' to
``compensator matrix''.  It overlooked that layer v4.9.0-r05 had already, at the author's request of 21 October 2026, named
\(\mathcal M_{16}\) the \emph{layer-transport matrix} and its restriction to the \((E,B)\)-doublet the \emph{port-weighting
matrix}, without changing the main body.  This layer corrects the record.  Earlier layers are not rewritten.

\noindent\textbf{Source-only status.}
This v4.9.0-r76 layer is source-only.  It does not build a PDF, does not assign a new DOI,
and does not authorize a public release.  The v4.9.0 Version DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23534954}{10.5281/zenodo.23534954} and the Concept DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19210728}{10.5281/zenodo.19210728} are unchanged by it.

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r76 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r76-results}

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r76.1 (correction of r75).}
The statement of r75 that \(\mathcal M_{16}\) ``was called the Millennium matrix'' is incomplete: from r05 to r75 the
declared name was ``layer-transport matrix'', while the main body still said ``Millennium matrix''.  The author confirmed on
3 November 2026, after being shown r05, that \emph{compensator matrix} is the valid name.  It replaces ``layer-transport
matrix''.  Reason: the defining properties of \(\mathcal M_{16}\) in \S\ref{sec:millennium-hc} are that it extends
\(C=\tfrac12(\mathrm{id}+J)\) to all layers and has \(C\) as a spectral projector; ``layer transport'' describes only its place
in the master chain.

\paragraph{Record v4.9.0-r76.2 (doublet restriction).}
The name \emph{port-weighting matrix} of r05 for the restriction of \(\mathcal M_{16}\) to the \((E,B)\)-doublet stays.  It
keeps the compensator matrix \(\mathcal M_{16}\) apart from the \(2\times2\) matrix of the single compensator on the doublet
(v4.9.0-r03).

\paragraph{Reading rule.}
In earlier text ``Millennium matrix'' and, in layers r05 to r74, ``layer-transport matrix'' both mean the compensator matrix
\(\mathcal M_{16}\).  Symbol, labels and the abbreviation MM are unchanged (r75.1).  No mathematical statement changes.
