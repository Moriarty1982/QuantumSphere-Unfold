\subsection*{v4.9.0-r53 couplings}
\label{subsec:v490-r53-couplings}

The triality form \(t(x,y,z)=\operatorname{Re}((xy)z)\) of v4.9.0-r37 couples only different slots.  A coupling of two generations with a Higgs value
\(v_k\) in the third slot is
\[
T(v)=\begin{pmatrix}0&v_3&v_2\\v_3&0&v_1\\v_2&v_1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad v\in\mathbb R^3 .
\]
The other invariant of the structure, the norm on each slot, gives a diagonal part \(\alpha\cdot1\) equal on all slots.  Tested were (a) \(T(v)\)
alone, with or without the mask in form K; (b) \(\alpha\cdot1+T(v)\) without mask, with one free parameter per sector left after fitting the
masses; (c) the circulant couplings with mask of v4.9.0-r52, with the sectors carrying \(\tilde H\) (\(u\), \(\nu\)) in one form and those
carrying \(H\) (\(d\), \(e\)) in the other.  Data as in v4.9.0-r50, and \(\sum m_\nu<0.0642\) eV (DESI DR2 BAO with CMB).

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r53 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r53-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r53.1.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] \(T(v)\) and \(DT(v)D\) are traceless, so the moduli of their eigenvalues satisfy \(m_3=m_1+m_2\).  For \(u\), \(d\), \(e\) this fails by
\(99.6\%\), \(98.0\%\), \(94.1\%\) of \(m_3\).  For Dirac neutrinos in normal ordering it holds only at \(m_1^*=0.028\) eV, where
\(\sum m_\nu=0.115\) eV.
\item[(b)] With \(\alpha\cdot1+T(v)\) no pair of quark solutions gives \(|V_{us}|\), \(|V_{cb}|\), \(|V_{ub}|\) within \(\pm20\%\) (closest case
\(82\%\) off; \(80\%\) with dense sampling).  Lepton pairs reach the \(3\sigma\) ranges in \(264\) of \(13\,651\,456\) cases, all at \(m_1=0.05\) or
\(0.1\) eV.
\item[(c)] Neither assignment of \(H\) and \(\tilde H\) to the forms K and S gives the CKM values within \(\pm20\%\) (closest cases \(37\%\) and
\(40\%\)).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
(a) \(\operatorname{tr}T(v)=0\) and \(\operatorname{tr}DT(v)D=\sum_iD_i^2T_{ii}=0\); the root \(m_1^*\) and the rest numerically (script
\texttt{llm/k93\_test\_h37.py}).  The spectrum of \(\alpha\cdot1+T(v)\) depends only on \(\alpha\), \(\sum v_k^2\) and \(v_1v_2v_3\), which gives the
solution curves used in (b).  The dense sampling in (b) and the cosmological reading of the lepton cases are checks after the evaluation.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H37a is refuted, H37b is a partial finding by the criterion (leptons only), H37c is refuted.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item A coupling from the triality form alone is forbidden: it would require \(m_3=m_1+m_2\) in every sector, which the charged masses violate and
which for neutrinos needs \(\sum m_\nu\approx0.115\) eV, excluded by cosmology.  The masses need a coupling within a slot.
\item The partial finding of (b) is not a physical one: the lepton cases need quasi-degenerate neutrinos with \(\sum m_\nu=0.17\) or \(0.31\) eV,
excluded by the same bound, and they are \(0.002\%\) of all pairs.  The quarks do not fit at all.
\item Closest to the data remains form K with circulant couplings (v4.9.0-r52, \(36\%\)).  None of the couplings suggested by the structure explains
the mixing; its origin remains open.
\end{enumerate}
