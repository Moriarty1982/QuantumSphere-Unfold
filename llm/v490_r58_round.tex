\subsection*{v4.9.0-r58 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r58-results}

Models, grids and assignments as in v4.9.0-r57; \(\delta\) from the Jarlskog invariant and \(|U_{\mu1}|^2\) for every case that fits \(\theta_{12}\) (JUNO \(2\sigma\)), \(\theta_{13}\)
and \(\theta_{23}\) (NuFIT \(3\sigma\)).  Pre-registered: a prediction if \(|\sin\delta|\) or \(\cos\delta\) lies in a range narrower than \(0.3\).

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r58.1 (numerical).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Variant A (\(776\) cases): \(|\sin\delta|\in[0.174,0.999]\), \(\cos\delta\in[-0.980,0.985]\).
\item[(b)] Variant B (\(3\,808\) cases): \(|\sin\delta|\in[0.172,0.999]\), \(\cos\delta\in[-0.981,0.985]\).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Script \texttt{llm/k98\_test\_h43.py}, output \texttt{llm/k98\_out.txt}; it reproduces the case counts of v4.9.0-r57.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H43a and H43b are refuted; no prediction for \(\delta\) is registered.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The reading does not fix \(\delta\); only phases very close to \(0^\circ\) or \(180^\circ\) (\(|\sin\delta|<0.17\)) do not occur, a weak restriction that may depend on
the grid.
\item The corrected trimaximal reading remains a fit with two settings: \(\theta_{12}\) fits, \(\theta_{23}\) is bounded from above (pending prediction H42), \(\delta\) is free.  A
sharper reading would need the correction itself fixed by the structure.
\end{enumerate}
