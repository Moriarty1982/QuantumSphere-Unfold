\subsection*{v4.9.0-r60 idea}
\label{subsec:v490-r60-idea}

The mask enters as \(D=\exp(\varepsilon W)\) with real diagonal \(W\): a hyperbolic, boost-like map of the generation slots, whose natural parameter is a rapidity.  The
theory has one such quantity, the rapidity \(\eta\) of the matter frame, \(\beta_{\rm sym}=\tanh\eta\), \(\gamma_L=\cosh2\eta\) (Convention~F).  Reading:
\(\varepsilon=\eta\).  Values: \(\operatorname{artanh}(0.5493)=0.6174\) (measured \(\beta_{\rm sym}\)) and \(\operatorname{artanh}(1/\sqrt3)=0.6585\) (Hopf value).

\subsection*{v4.9.0-r60 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r60-results}

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r60.1 (numerical).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] At \(\varepsilon=0.6174\) variant A of v4.9.0-r57 has \(120\) and variant B \(1\,104\) cases with \(\theta_{12}\) in the JUNO \(2\sigma\) range and \(\theta_{13}\),
\(\theta_{23}\) in the NuFIT \(3\sigma\) ranges.
\item[(b)] In both variants these cases have \(\sin^2\theta_{23}\in[0.4913,0.4975]\) and \(|\sin\delta|\in[0.17,1.00]\).
\item[(c)] At the Hopf value \(\varepsilon=0.6585\) there are no such cases.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
Script \texttt{llm/k100\_test\_h45.py}, output \texttt{llm/k100\_out.txt}.  \(\square\)

\paragraph{Verdict and reading.}
H45a is confirmed as compatibility only (not blind); H45b, which required both \(\theta_{23}\) and \(\delta\) to be sharp, is refuted, because \(\delta\) stays free.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item For the first time a reading takes \(\varepsilon\) from the structure and meets the data: the measured frame offset fits, the Hopf value does not.
\item New and unknown before: with \(\varepsilon=\operatorname{artanh}\beta_{\rm sym}\) the reading fixes \(\sin^2\theta_{23}\approx0.49\)--\(0.50\), near maximal mixing and just in the
lower octant, in both variants.  This is sharper than the pending prediction H42 (\(\le0.54\)).  By the criterion of H45 it is not registered as a prediction; a
prediction for \(\theta_{23}\) alone would need its own note.
\item Limits: not blind; \(\beta_{\rm sym}\) itself is an input (OP1 open); \(\varepsilon=\eta\) is a reading, not derived from an action.
\end{enumerate}
