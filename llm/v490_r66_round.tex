\subsection*{v4.9.0-r66 results}
\label{subsec:v490-r66-results}

\paragraph{Correction v4.9.0-r66.1.}
The contradiction of r64.1(a), \(k(\gBI)=0\) in Theorem~\ref{thm:k-values} against Theorem~\ref{thm:frame-offset}, is item~1 of the two contradictions of
v4.9.0-r09 (\S\ref{sec:v490-r09-local-frame-factor}).  r09 resolved both by reading Theorem~\ref{thm:frame-offset} as a statement about the structural factor
\(\gL\) alone, with the drifting local factor \(\gL^{(\mathrm{local})}\) of r08 and r18 kept separate.  r64.1(a) is therefore not new, and its phrase ``independently of
r63'' should have cited r09.  The status note of r65 is extended with the reference.  r64.1(b) to (h) are unaffected: they concern the structural factor.

\paragraph{Theoremlet v4.9.0-r66.2 (local factor).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[(a)] Under the counting reading the structural factor is the ratio of two counting solutions (r63.1), a fixed number.  This agrees with r08, which already
called it fixed because both inputs are fixed numbers.
\item[(b)] The two rules of r63 are discrete: spin \(\tfrac12\) only, or all spins with weight \(2\).  They admit no continuous family, so the counting reading gives no
mechanism for a continuously drifting local factor \(\gL^{(\mathrm{local})}(t)\).  A continuous interpolation of the weights would be a new assumption.
\item[(c)] The drift readings tested in r08 and r09 are excluded by atomic clocks; r66.2(b) adds that the counting reading does not supply them either.  The
corollary ``\(\alpha\)-drift as a frame effect'' of \S\ref{sec:phase-constants} still carries no status note; it is a candidate.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.}
r66.1 by reading r09; r66.2 from the counting equations of r63.1.  \(\square\)
