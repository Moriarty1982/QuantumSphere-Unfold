## What is new

- Full Companion: research layers v4.10.0-r29 and r30. r29 records the counting rule of the matter frame as one number in
  several equivalent forms (flux lines of an isolated horizon, readout frequency, a Moran-equation reading, two readings of
  the frame register) with three explicit withdrawals. r30 records that a unitary boost leaves the area spectrum unchanged,
  so a frame-dependent area quantum would need a non-unitary frame map; that the rule of the second frame has no data-free
  reason; a chance estimate under which the hits of the frame factor are compatible with chance once the choice of the
  counting-rule pair is counted; a map of how one number enters the other open problems through five separately chosen
  translations, and a mediator profile that no recorded candidate meets; five pre-registered, not blind tests on the bent
  octant (H113–H117); and the OP5 explorations and the comparison of the tape machine in two hardware descriptions with the
  database calculus.
- Physics paper: two development subsections, "Orientation of the octant step" (5.9) and "One number and several
  translations" (7.7), one sentence each in the main text, and the reference Rovelli–Speziale 2003.
- Research catalogue: six new entries in `qam/editions/v5_research_notes.json`.

## Scope

This minor version changes content, not the status of the open physical questions. The orientation of the octant step,
the size of the core, the counting rule per frame and the translations of the one number remain open. No guard or open
problem is closed by the release. Database records and hashes do not prove physical interpretation.
