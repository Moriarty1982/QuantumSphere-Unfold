# Changes planned for v5.0.0

Unpublished preparation. Comparison baseline: v4.11.0, released
10 November 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24108818.

## Document architecture

- Statements, definitions, source identities and immutable revisions are
  organized in a SQLite/SQLAlchemy store, with explicit dependencies,
  remaining guards and an append-only ledger.
- The initial inventory contains 2468 statement environments from 61 source
  files. Registration preserves the imported review status; it does not
  turn inherited theorem headings into verified statements.
- Ordered editions and registered transition/explanatory text assemble
  LaTeX selections. Snapshots and source hashes bind each edition to its
  inputs; the full edition retains the complete modular source tree.
- A shared build produces the full Companion and the selected English
  physics paper from one snapshot, with manifests, checksums and a portable
  database. Linux/Windows source identity and container builds are covered.

## English physics paper

The new standalone paper is titled *Graded carriers and physical readouts:
conditions for mass and geometric interpretation*. It reorganizes selected
existing results into a connected argument rather than reproducing the
chronological research protocols.

- Carrier, scalar field, adjoint, grading and physical interpretation are
  distinguished. The compensator projection is proved under its stated
  orthogonal-involution assumptions.
- QAM, MML and MMA are explained through a classical electromagnetic
  example, including information lost by a reduced readout, an exact
  interval calculation and a stored execution receipt.
- Conditional particle assignments are separated from generation seeds,
  rank requirements, eigenmasses, slot readouts and relative mixing bases.
- The Koide/HC branch states its additional norm/orthogonality condition,
  positive-domain restrictions, antipodal bound and fit freedom. A gapped
  comparison distinguishes leading Gram laws from exact dressed states.
  Phase overlap distinguishes root averaging, mass averaging and coherent
  operator coupling. The overlap analysis was already present in v4.11.0;
  its inclusion here is a new presentation, not a newly established result.
- Projected dynamics exposes hidden-lift dependence and memory. Modal
  inertia is separated from dissipation. Coframe and finite-jet comparisons
  retain the distinction between compatibility and regional field existence.
- A zeta/parity comparison at sigma greater than one constructs a regular
  balanced spectral packet compatible with uncertainty. It supplies no
  physical preparation law, three-lepton readout or critical-line claim.

## Editorial and technical work

- Wide historical boxes and six tables were reflowed, and long protocol
  names made breakable, preserving scientific text and historical guards.
- The inspected container draft has 1401 full-document pages and 27
  physics-paper pages; local pagination differs. Both logs have zero
  undefined references, duplicate labels, rerun requests and overfull boxes.
- The physics manuscript was self-reviewed with an explicit source snapshot,
  coverage record and limitations. No independent peer review is claimed.
- A 29-page German reader edition exists separately as a reading aid;
  the automatic paired release currently targets the two English documents.

## Scientific status retained

Version 5 marks an organizational change. It does not close OP1–OP5,
C5 or C6/T2, select a native mass operator, predict the measured lepton
masses or fix an absolute mass scale. Existing local, restricted,
effective and comparison-dependent results keep their assumptions.
Both editions retain open argument-review obligations. A successful
build is not a proof certificate or a publication decision.
