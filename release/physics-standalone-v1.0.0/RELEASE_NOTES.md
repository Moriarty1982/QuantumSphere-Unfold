## Scope

No lepton masses, no absolute scale and no value of Λ are derived, and no blind prediction has so far been confirmed against measurement data. The open problems OP1–OP5 and the testable statements are listed in the article with their status; none of them is presented as settled. The authoritative source of every statement is the Quantum Sphaera Companion (concept doi:10.5281/zenodo.19210728; baseline v5.4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24500009, and the research rounds up to v4.10.0-r61 in the public repository). This standalone article does not replace the physics paper bundled with Companion v5.4.0.
