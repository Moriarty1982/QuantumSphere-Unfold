\section*{Results, Conditions, and Reading Route}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Results, Conditions, and Reading Route}
\label{v480:reading-route}

This guide identifies which results can be used in the development
manuscript, what their physical interpretations require, and where later
corrections qualify claims retained from earlier versions. The published
baseline is v5.2.0
(\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24294648}{Version DOI}).

\subsection*{Mathematical results to start with}

\paragraph{Compensator and sector structure.}
On its declared carrier, the average of the identity and a linear unitary involution
is a projector onto the fixed sector. The algebraic decomposition
and the proposed identification of its sectors with physical states
have separate premises.
\hyperref[subsec:v480-r04-standalone-compensator]{Standalone compensator argument}.

\paragraph{Quotient and readout factorization.}
A total readout constant on the declared equivalence classes factors
uniquely through their quotient. A partial readout also needs a
class-saturated domain for the factored map to reproduce it as a partial
function. The relation and the admitted fragment are supplied.
Propositions~\ref{v480:kernel-quotient} and
\ref{v480:kernel-partial-factorization}.

\paragraph{Operator compression and spectral shape.}
The minimal defect penalty compresses to a Gram matrix for a closed
isometric embedding. For nonnegative principal mass roots, the Koide ratio equals $2/3$ exactly when the
singlet weight is one half of the total squared mass-root norm in the
orthogonal singlet--doublet split. Evaluating the physical matrix
elements and explaining the norm balance are separate tasks.
Propositions~\ref{v480:native-penalty-identity} and
\ref{v480:koide-equal-norm}.

\subsection*{Physical identifications that remain open}

\paragraph{Native operators.}
The conditional MM/HC spectral identification requires scalar action
on the selected subspace and exclusion of further vectors at that
eigenvalue in its complement. The native carrier, operator action and
joint matrix elements needed for the mass coupling remain to be
established.
Proposition~\ref{v480:conditional-mm} and
Section~\ref{v480:native-spectral-data}.

\paragraph{Physical mass scales.}
The gapped-block construction yields a conditional effective mass
operator. Its scale and the physical readout need independent
selection. In the tested fixed-shape candidate, changing a common
positive scale leaves the failed mass ratio unchanged.
Propositions~\ref{v480:native-schur-mass}
and~\ref{v480:coupling-gap-ratio-obstruction}.

\paragraph{Field equations and closure.}
The neutral Cartan comparison determines source values from compatible
tangential jets in a declared finite system. Regional existence,
a native action and unrestricted closure require further arguments.
A positive residual below a C5 detector tolerance does not establish
exact finite zero. The unrestricted all-readout and C6/T2 claims
remain open. Proposition~\ref{v490:source-law-theorem} and
Sections~\ref{v490:cartan-evidence} and~\ref{v480:op5-premises}.

\subsection*{Earlier claims and their present status}
\label{v5:earlier-claims}

The source retains earlier statements with their numbering for
traceability. A status note or a later round may restrict the use of a
statement that still appears under its original theorem label.

\paragraph{Unity and the Riemann route.}
The Stone--von Neumann justification was withdrawn. The physical
unity equivalence remains conjectural. The inherited GUE derivation
and its use to determine the frame factor are archived.
\hyperref[sec:v480-r04-riemann-decoupling]{Riemann decoupling, r04}
and \hyperref[sec:v490-archive]{Archived Statements of the Main Body}.

\paragraph{Unqualified MM/HC spectral identification.}
Commutation alone preserves the selected subspace. The stronger
eigenprojection claim needs the additional premises above. The
premise audit gives counterexamples and a sufficient replacement.
Section~\ref{v480:spectral-premises}.

\paragraph{The frame factor and the inverse exponent.}
The inverse-exponent relation is an identity. A separately fixed
ansatz gives a conditional root. The later counting interpretation
still requires a justification for the matter-frame counting rule.
Section~\ref{v480:exponent-premises} and
\hyperref[sec:v4100-r28-bend-counting-status]{counting-rule status, r28}.

\paragraph{Koide from the carrier alone.}
The historical derivation is archived. The exact norm condition
survives as a mathematical result. A structural origin for the
physical mass roots and the bending phase remains open.
\hyperref[sec:v490-archive]{Archived Koide statement},
Section~\ref{v480:koide-condition}, and
\hyperref[sec:v4100-r13-neutral-qubit-family]{carrier comparison, r13}.

\paragraph{OP5 and the cosmological readout.}
The historical bare-vacuum and $\Theta$-sum route is suspended as a
foundation. The tested full-sum conjecture was refuted. The guarded
local closure question is distinct from the present-epoch
$\Omega_M$ prediction H96, which remains open.
Section~\ref{v480:op5-premises} and
\hyperref[sec:v4100-r05-core-review]{separation of the OP5 questions, r05}.
