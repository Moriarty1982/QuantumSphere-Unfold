\section{QAM, MML and MMA: preserved formal-language definitions}
\label{app:qam-runtime-details}

This appendix is the definition-level target of
\hyperref[sec:compact-qam-interface]{the compact main-text interface}.
The five source modules below
are included unchanged and exactly once. Their historical orientation
paragraphs describe the inherited reading sequence; the current main-text
route is the compact interface. Moving their types, predicates and command
semantics here changes neither their content nor their proof obligations.

\paragraph{Definition and execution are different.}
The specified instruction alphabet is a formal interface. It is not a
claim that every named operation has a running implementation or that any
stored witness is correct. The executed numerical and grammar checks are
identified separately in Appendix~\ref{app:verification-ledger}.
No unavailable guard, closure certificate or machine proof is inferred
from a command name.
