\section*{The mathematical route and its formal-language interface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The mathematical route and its formal-language interface}
\label{sec:compact-qam-interface}

The main argument follows the Sphaera construction through MM transport,
HC sector selection and physical readouts to the remaining OP burdens.
Three distinctions govern that route: an invariant sector need not be
a single eigenspace; a calibrated numerical fit is not a structural
derivation; and a finite detector result is not exact global closure.
Definitions and theorem hypotheses remain binding wherever a later
readout uses them.

\paragraph{What QAM, MML and MMA contribute.}
QAM codes states, operators, readouts and witnesses as set-theoretic
objects. MML names operations on those objects; MMA specifies guarded
transitions and return statuses. These layers record and transport
mathematical data. They do not make a stored assertion true, identify
an unspecified residual or prove that a physical state satisfies a guard.

For precise use, the unchanged formal definitions are collected in
Appendix~\ref{app:qam-runtime-details}. In particular,
Definition~\ref{def:qam-store-state} fixes the store state,
Definition~\ref{def:mml-store-instructions} the
\texttt{STORE}, \texttt{LINK}, \texttt{SNAPSHOT}, \texttt{CLONE},
\texttt{ROLLBACK} and \texttt{SCRUB}
alphabet, and Definition~\ref{def:mma-store-transition} its guarded
transition semantics. The same appendix retains the coded predicates,
mixed-readout rules, masks and translation clauses. Current pre-v4 tags
are unchanged; relocating their definitions performs no Hilbert shift.

\paragraph{How to read the core.}
For the MM/HC identification, use the explicit spectral hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:hc-spectral-projector} and the proof in
Appendix~\ref{app:core-proof-details}. For OP1, distinguish the assumed
fixed-exponent equation of Theorem~\ref{thm:self-consistent-gL} from
the inverse diagnostic in Proposition~\ref{prop:exponent-5}. For OP5,
start at the open balance condition in Definition~\ref{def:tr-balance}
and its status guide, Remark~\ref{rem:op5-reading-contract}.

\paragraph{What a short reference means.}
A citation or guarded token points to specified hypotheses, a witness
and a status; it does not run a background verifier. Complete proofs,
counterexamples and the limits of the executed checks are available in
Appendix~\ref{app:core-proof-details}. Formal command typing and a
successful LaTeX build are not substitutes for those proofs. This
reading-route change does not declare a new release or OP closure.
