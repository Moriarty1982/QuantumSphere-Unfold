\section*{Reader Orientation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reader Orientation}
\begin{quote}\small
\noindent\textbf{Purpose.} The main reading route follows the construction, its mathematical conditions and its unresolved physical identifications. The appendices supply the full supporting detail.
\end{quote}

Begin with the compact QAM interface, nomenclature and Sphaera construction. The MM/HC and OP~1 sections state their hypotheses and distinguish conditional conclusions from physical derivations; the OP~5 reading contract identifies the outstanding balance and limiting obligations.

For proof details and counterexamples, use Appendix~\ref{app:core-proof-details}. For the full QAM coding, guarded transitions, planned Hilbert-hotel reindexing, mixed-readout packages and mask registry, use Appendix~\ref{app:qam-runtime-details}. Current inhabited tags, including $\langle0,a\rangle$, remain unchanged; mask value zero belongs to a separate optional registry.

The local crystal repair, HC handoff, coupled OP-entry and branch-shell transport protocols are preserved in Appendices~\ref{app:local-crystal-protocol}, \ref{app:hc-transport-protocols}, \ref{app:local-op-entry} and~\ref{app:op-transport-unfolding}. Historical status records and the detailed computational specification are separated from the live burden ledger; their original conditional or historical status remains binding.

Two-sided indices are a notation guide: front indices mark readout roles and back indices structural roles. Ordinary contraction is unchanged; same-variance pairings still require the explicit comparison or selector tensors specified in the formal appendix.

\paragraph{Status.}
OP~1/4 and OP~5(B) remain open. QAM reindexing is planned but not executed. Primitive mixed tensors remain deferred. The two-sided notation is only a conservative compression grammar. The Sphaera array parity/residual layer remains diagnostic infrastructure, not a closure theorem. Turing-completeness remains a later machine-semantics question.

\paragraph{Reader route.}
Read an appendix reference as a recoverable source of definitions, assumptions and arguments, not as a background computation. Compilation checks document processing; numerical regression tests check their stated finite examples. Neither establishes the unresolved physical claims.
